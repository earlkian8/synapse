One SYNAPSE account can belong to several companies — for example if you work for two employers, or look after more than one business. Each company is its own **workspace**, with its own people, records, settings and roles. Nothing is shared between them, and your role in one says nothing about your role in another.

## Choosing a workspace when you sign in

If you belong to more than one company, signing in opens **Choose a workspace**: a card for each company with your role there and its headcount. Pick one to go to its dashboard. If you belong to only one company, you go straight in.

## Switching

The company name at the top of the sidebar is a **workspace switcher**. Open it and pick another company — everything on screen switches to that company. **Browse all workspaces** returns to the picker.

## Creating a workspace

If you run HR for another company too, add it to the account you already have: open the workspace switcher and choose **Create workspace**, then enter the company's name. (It is also at the end of the workspace picker and in **Settings** → **Workspaces**.) You become the new company's HR Manager, and its setup opens so you can fill in the basics. Your sign-in stays the same.

> [!NOTE]
> Everything follows the workspace you are in: the sidebar, the dashboard, the assistant, and this Help Center (which shows the articles your role **in that company** allows).

## Joining another company

Join from the mobile app with the invitation you were sent, or your new employer's join code — the same way you joined your first. See [Joining your company from the app](/help/mobile-app/joining-your-company-on-mobile).

## Leaving a company

When you stop working for a company — you resigned, or your contract ended — open **Settings** → **Workspaces** and choose **Leave** beside it. Your roles there end, and so does your access to its records. Your account, your place in any other company, and the company's employee record of you stay as they are. To come back, someone there has to invite you again.

The company's HR can also remove you, from **User Management**. You get a notification when they do.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> If you are the company's **only HR Manager** and other people still work there, you can't leave yet, and you can't delete your account. Make someone else an HR Manager first, in that company's **User Management**. Otherwise nobody would be left who can run it.

If nobody else is in a workspace, you can still leave it, but no one can open it again afterwards. You type its name to confirm.

## If you belong to no company

If you have left your last company, or registered in the mobile app and not joined one yet, signing in on the web shows **You are not in a workspace**. Create one there, or join your employer's from the app.
