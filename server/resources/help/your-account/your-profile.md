Your settings live under your account menu (your photo or initials, top right) → **Settings**. They belong to you, not to your company, so they are the same in every workspace you belong to.

## Your name and email address

**Settings** → **Profile** holds your first, middle and last name and your email address. Change them and choose **Save**.

Changing your email address means confirming the new one before you carry on, with a 6-digit code sent to it — the same way you confirmed your address when you created your account. Choose **Send a new code** on the confirmation screen if one hasn't arrived.

> [!NOTE]
> Your employee record — the 201 file your HR team keeps — is separate from your account. Ask HR to correct details there, such as your position or department.

## Light, dark or system

**Settings** → **Appearance** offers **Light**, **Dark** and **System** (follow your device's setting). The sun/moon button in the top bar switches between light and dark from any page. The choice is remembered on the device you made it on.

## Deleting your account

At the bottom of **Settings** → **Profile**, **Delete account** removes your account for good. You are asked for your password to confirm.

> [!WARNING]
> Deleting your account cannot be undone. If you only want to stop working in one company, ask that company's administrator to remove you from its workspace instead.
