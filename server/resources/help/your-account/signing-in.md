## Creating an account

There are two ways to get a SYNAPSE account, depending on who you are:

- **You are setting up a company.** Choose **Create account** on the sign-in screen. Along with your name, email address and a password, you name your company — this creates a new, empty workspace with you as its HR Manager. You are then taken through the [Setup Guide](/help/getting-started/setting-up-your-company).
- **You are joining your employer.** Create your account in the SYNAPSE mobile app, then join your company with the invitation you were sent or your company's join code. See [Joining your company from the app](/help/mobile-app/joining-your-company-on-mobile).

> [!WARNING]
> Don't use **Create account** on the web to join your employer — it creates a separate company of your own.

## Confirming your email address

A new account has to confirm its email address before it can be used. SYNAPSE emails you a **6-digit code**; type it into the boxes and it is checked as soon as the last digit is in — there is no button to press.

If it doesn't arrive, check your spam folder, then choose **Send a new code**. Each new code replaces the previous one, so always use the latest.

## Signing in

Sign in with your **email address and password**. If you have added a **passkey** — your device's fingerprint, face or PIN — you can sign in with it instead, without typing a password. See [Password, two-factor and passkeys](/help/your-account/password-and-security).

If you have turned on **two-factor authentication**, you are then asked for the 6-digit code from your authenticator app. Lost your phone? Use one of your recovery codes instead.

If you belong to more than one company, you then choose which one to work in. See [Working in more than one company](/help/your-account/working-in-more-than-one-company).

## Forgot your password?

Choose **Forgot your password?** on the sign-in screen and enter your email address. SYNAPSE emails you a link to choose a new one.

Nobody at your company can see your password, set it, or reset it for you — only you can, from this link.

## Signing out

Open your account menu (your photo or initials, top right) and choose **Log out**.

## If you can't sign in

- **"These credentials do not match our records"** — check the email address for typos, then reset your password.
- **"This account is inactive"** — an administrator in your company has deactivated your account. Ask them to reactivate it.
