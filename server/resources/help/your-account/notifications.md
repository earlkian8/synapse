SYNAPSE tells you when something needs you or concerns you — a leave request to decide, a task assigned to you, an event invitation, a reminder to clock in, an announcement from your company. Notifications always arrive in the app, and can also reach you by email and as desktop notifications.

## The bell

The bell in the top bar shows your latest notifications, with a dot when something is unread. Click a notification to open what it is about; it is marked as read as you go. **Mark all read** clears the dot, and **View all notifications** opens the full list.

## Your notification centre

**System** → **Notifications** in the sidebar (also **View all notifications** under the bell) keeps everything that has been sent to you:

- switch between **All** and **Unread**;
- mark one as read, or **Mark all read**;
- delete one, or **Clear all** (you are asked to confirm);
- click one to go to what it is about.

## Choosing how notifications reach you

At the top of the notification centre:

- **Email notifications** — also send important notifications to your email inbox.
- **Desktop notifications** — push alerts to this computer, even when SYNAPSE isn't open. Your browser asks for permission the first time you turn it on. It is set **per device**: turn it on in each browser you use.

Notifications always appear in the app, whatever you choose here.

> [!TIP]
> If desktop notifications don't appear, check that your browser and your operating system both allow notifications from SYNAPSE.

## What you may be notified about

Depending on your role and what you are involved in, for example:

- **Leave** — a new request waiting for a decision, or a decision on yours.
- **Attendance** — a reminder to clock in, if your company's attendance policy sends one; and, for people who review attendance, each day's exceptions once the day has closed.
- **Recruitment** — a new application, or a hire.
- **Onboarding** — a task assigned to you, or a reminder about an overdue one.
- **Events** — an invitation, or a reminder to reply.
- **Your account** — a role you have been given, and other changes to your access.
- **Announcements** from your company — see [Sending announcements](/help/administration/sending-announcements).
