SYNAPSE holds personal information — your account, and the HR records your organisation keeps about you. This article explains who is responsible for it and what you can ask for. The full details are in the [Privacy Policy](/privacy) and the [Terms of Service](/terms), linked at the bottom of every page.

## Who is responsible

- **Your HR records** — your 201 file, attendance, leave, appraisals and the rest — are your **employer's**. It decides what is recorded and who in the company can see it, and it is responsible for them under the Data Privacy Act.
- **Your account** — your sign-in details and settings — and how SYNAPSE itself runs are the responsibility of the company that operates SYNAPSE.

Inside your workspace, each person sees only what their role allows, and every change is recorded in an audit trail.

## What you can ask for

Under the Data Privacy Act you can ask to **see** your information and get a copy, have it **corrected**, have it **deleted or blocked** when it shouldn't be kept, **object** to how it's used, and receive it in a **portable** format. You can also complain to the National Privacy Commission.

- **Your name and email address** — change them yourself in **Settings** → **Profile**. See [Your profile and appearance](/help/your-account/your-profile).
- **Your account** — delete it yourself at the bottom of **Settings** → **Profile**.
- **Your HR records** — ask your HR team first. They can correct most records directly.
- **Anything else**, or if you aren't satisfied — write to the privacy contact named in the [Privacy Policy](/privacy).

## AI and predictions

Some features use AI, and SYNAPSE can make predictions such as attrition risk. No decision about you is made by a machine alone, and the assistant never discloses pay, government ID numbers, bank details, home addresses or dates of birth in chat. See [What the assistant will and will not do](/help/assistant/assistant-privacy-and-confirmations) and section 5 of the [Privacy Policy](/privacy).

> [!NOTE]
> Applying for a job on a careers page doesn't need an account. The organisation you apply to receives your application, as its form explains.
