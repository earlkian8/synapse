**Company Setup** → **Locations** is where your people work: each **site** is a place on a map with a **fence** around it — a circle, or a shape you draw — that clock-ins are checked against, and the clock it keeps.

## The map

Every active site is drawn on the map with its fence, above a table of sites: each with its address, its fence, the clock it keeps, how many people are based there, and the schedule and policy it gives them. Click a row to find the site on the map.

If an attendance policy checks where people punch but you haven't drawn a single site yet, the page warns you that nothing is being checked.

## Adding a site

1. Choose **New location**.
2. Place it: **search for an address**, choose **Use my location**, or click the map. Drag the pin to adjust, or type the latitude and longitude.
3. Choose its fence:
    - **Circle** — set the **radius**, from 25 metres to 5 kilometres (150 metres to start). The fence is drawn as you slide.
    - **Draw a shape** — for a site no circle fits, such as a long warehouse or a campus along a road. Click the map at each corner, in order round the site; the last corner joins the first. Drag a corner to move it; **Undo corner** and **Clear** start over. A shape needs at least three corners, can't cross itself, and must fit within 5 kilometres of its centre.
4. Give it a name and address and, optionally, a **default schedule** and **attendance policy** for the people based there.
5. If the site is in another time zone, turn on **Keeps its own clock** and pick the zone.

## A site's own clock

Every day is judged on the company's clock unless the person's base site keeps its own. Then their **late**, their **today**, and which day a punch belongs to are all read on the site's clock — a 9:00 start in Tokyo is 9:00 Tokyo time. The day remembers the clock it was judged on, so its times are shown on that clock on the attendance board and in the app.

## Who is based where

Open a site's **People based here** to tick who works there. Star one site as a person's **primary** site — they can have only one.

A person is checked against the fences of the sites they are based at. Someone based nowhere is checked against every active site — so a company with one office just draws it, and is done.

## What happens to a punch outside the fence

That's the [attendance policy](/help/company-setup/attendance-policies)'s choice:

- **Off** — the punch is recorded, with the nearest site and the distance;
- **Flag** — it's accepted, and the day waits for a manager's sign-off;
- **Block** — it's refused, and the person is told the nearest site and how far away they are.

Only punches from the web and the mobile app are checked; times HR enters by hand aren't. A phone's location has some uncertainty, and a punch counts as inside when it could be inside. For a shape, the distance is measured to its nearest edge.

## Archiving and deleting

**Archive** a site to stop checking punches against it. Punches made there keep naming it. A site that any punch names can't be deleted permanently — it stays archived, so past days can still say where their punches were.

> [!NOTE]
> Moving or archiving a site doesn't re-judge past punches. A punch is judged where it was made.
