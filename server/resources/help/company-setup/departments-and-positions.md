**Company Setup** → **Departments** is your org structure: the departments your company is divided into, how they nest, who heads each one, and the **positions** (job titles) in each. Employee records, job postings and appraisal frameworks all choose from these.

## The department tree

Departments are shown as a tree, because that's what they are: a top-level department can hold sub-departments, and those can hold their own. Each shows its code, its head, and how many employees and positions it has. Search to find one quickly.

## Adding and editing a department

Choose to add a department and give it:

- a **name**, and a short **code** (for example `FIN`) — unique in your company and shown in capitals;
- a **parent** department, or none for a top-level one. A department can't be moved under itself or one of its own sub-departments;
- a **head** — one of your employees;
- a description.

## Positions

Select a department to open its details, where its positions are listed. Add, edit or delete positions there. A position can carry a **salary band** (minimum and maximum).

## Archiving and restoring

Archive a department you no longer use. Archived departments are listed separately, from where you can restore them — or delete them permanently, which detaches everyone from them.

Archiving frees the department's code. If another department has taken the code by the time you restore, you'll be asked to change one of them first.

> [!NOTE]
> A department can also set the default **work schedule** and **attendance policy** for its people. See [Work schedules and holidays](/help/company-setup/work-schedules-and-holidays) and [Attendance policies](/help/company-setup/attendance-policies).
