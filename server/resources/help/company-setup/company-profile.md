**Company Setup** → **Company Profile** is your company's own record. There is one per workspace.

## What it holds

- **Brand & identity** — your **logo** (shown in the sidebar; your initials stand in when there is none), your **display name**, and your **registered legal name**.
- **Contact details** — email, phone and address.
- **Time zone** — see below.
- **Government & statutory** — your employer numbers: **TIN**, **SSS**, **PhilHealth** and **Pag-IBIG**.

Change what you need and save. A logo can be a JPG, PNG, WebP or SVG of up to 2 MB.

People who can view the profile but not manage it see it read-only.

## Why the time zone matters

The time zone is the clock your whole company runs on:

- it decides who was **late**, and what "**today**" is on the attendance board;
- it decides which day a **night shift** belongs to;
- every attendance time is **shown** on it — on the web and in the mobile app — whatever time zone the reader is in;
- event times are entered and shown on it.

So a manager travelling abroad still sees the day exactly as it was judged. If your browser's zone differs from the company's, the form offers it with one click — but only change the company's zone if the company itself has moved.

> [!WARNING]
> Changing the time zone changes how new days are judged from then on. Days already recorded keep how they were judged.

## The join code

Your company's join code — what employees enter in the mobile app to ask to join — is managed on **Employees** → **App access**. See [Inviting your people](/help/getting-started/inviting-your-people).
