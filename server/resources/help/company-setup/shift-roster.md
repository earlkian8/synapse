**Company Setup** → **Shift Roster** is the **plan**: who is due to work what, day by day. Attendance is judged against it — the record of what actually happened is on the attendance board.

## Reading the roster

Employees run down the side and the week runs across. Each cell shows the shift that applies to that person on that day, with the schedule's name under it. A **pin** marks a one-off change. Move between weeks — including future ones, since planning ahead is the point — search, and filter by department.

Each cell knows **why** it says what it says: a one-off change, an assignment, the person's own schedule, their department's default, their location's default, or the company default.

## Assigning a schedule from a date

Use **Assign a schedule** to put people on a schedule from a chosen date — for example moving a team to the night shift from next Monday. You can also say which attendance policy their days should be judged by, if it should differ from the schedule's.

A rotating schedule can be offset per person, so two crews on the same rotation don't work the same days.

## Changing a single day

Click a cell to override that day for that person:

- another shift's hours for the day;
- hours of its own;
- a **day off**;

with a reason. To undo it, open the cell and choose **Clear override**.

> [!NOTE]
> Changing the plan for a day that has already passed updates how that day is judged, unless the person punched that day — a day someone worked keeps the schedule and policy it opened with.

## Who can do what

Viewing the roster and changing it (assigning schedules, overriding days) are separate permissions. A schedule can also be assigned from an employee's own profile by anyone who can edit employees.
