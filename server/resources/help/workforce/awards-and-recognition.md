**Workforce** → **Awards & Recognition** is where your company recognises great work, using the award types set up in [Award types](/help/company-setup/award-types).

## The recognition feed

The tiles count recognitions given in all and this month, the number of people recognised, and active award types. The table lists every award: the recipient, the award type, the reason, the date and who gave it. Filter by award type or search by employee. **Export** downloads it.

## Giving recognition

1. Choose **Give recognition**.
2. Pick the employee and the award type.
3. Write the **reason** — the citation people will read.
4. Choose the date. It can't be in the future.

Edit or remove an award from its row's **⋯** menu. Only active award types can be given; an award that already uses a retired type keeps it.

## The nomination board

**Nomination board** (at the top of the page, for people who can give recognition) ranks who leads for each award type. The first table lists each award type with what it weighs, its number of nominees and its front-runner. Open one to see its ranked **shortlist**: expand a row to see how that person's score was reached. The front-runner's breakdown opens first.

The nominations are a starting point for a decision, not the decision itself.

> [!NOTE]
> Every employee's profile has an **Awards** tab with their recognitions, and employees see their own in the mobile app.
