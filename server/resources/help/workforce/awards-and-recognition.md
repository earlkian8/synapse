**Workforce** → **Awards & Recognition** is where your company recognises great work, using the award types set up in [Award types](/help/company-setup/award-types). The buttons at the top right move between its sections; you see the ones your role includes:

- **Wall**, **My points** and **My nominations** — for everyone who takes part. See [Kudos, points and rewards](/help/workforce/kudos-points-and-rewards).
- **Awards**, **Nominations** and **Rewards** — for HR, described here.

## The awards register

**Awards** lists every award given. The tiles count recognitions given in all and this month, the number of people recognised, and active award types. The table shows the recipient, the award type, the reason, the date and who gave it. Filter by award type or search by employee. **Export** downloads it.

### Giving recognition

1. Choose **Give recognition**.
2. Pick the employee and the award type.
3. Write the **reason** — the citation people will read.
4. Choose the date. It can't be in the future.

The person is notified, and the award's **points** (set per award type) are added to their balance. Edit or remove an award from its row's **⋯** menu; changing its type or removing it corrects the points too. Only active award types can be given.

## Nominations

Colleagues nominate each other for award types that are open to nominations. **Nominations** lists the ones waiting, oldest first, with the reason the nominator gave. A count on the section shows how many wait.

- **Approve** gives the award. The citation starts as the nominator's reason; edit it, or ask for an AI draft when your workspace has AI turned on. The nominee gets the award and its points, and the nominator hears it was approved.
- **Turn down** closes it, with an optional note the nominator sees.

Nobody reviews a nomination they're part of — one they made, or one of themselves. Another reviewer decides those.

### AI shortlist

The **AI shortlist** view ranks who the records say deserves each award right now — from appraisals, the performance forecast, attendance, training, tenure and time since they were last recognised. Open an award type to see its ranked list and how each score was reached. It's a starting point for a decision, not the decision itself.

## Rewards

**Rewards** is the desk for what points buy:

- **Requests** — choose **Handed over** once the person has the reward, or **Decline** with a note; declining returns their points. You can't handle your own request.
- **Catalogue** — add a reward with its cost in points, a description and an optional stock. Archive one to stop offering it; requests already made stay.
- **Most points** — who holds the most. **Adjust points** adds or removes points for one person with a reason they see.
- **Kudos** — how many points a kudos gives, and how many kudos with points each person can send a month.

> [!NOTE]
> Every employee's profile has an **Awards** tab with their recognitions, and employees see their own in the mobile app.
