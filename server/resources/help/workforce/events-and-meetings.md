**Workforce** → **Events & Meetings** is your company calendar: town halls, trainings, team meetings and celebrations, with who is invited and who is coming.

## The events list

The tiles count upcoming events, events in all, meetings and invitations. The table lists each event with its kind and organiser, schedule, location, how many are attending out of those invited, and its status. Search, filter by kind and status, and sort.

An event's status comes from its times: **Upcoming** before it starts, **Ongoing** while it runs, and **Past** once it ends.

## Scheduling an event

Choose **New event** and give it a title, its kind — an **Event** or a **Meeting** — a description, location, and start and end times. Times are on **your company's clock**, so everyone sees the same time whatever their own time zone. You are recorded as the organiser.

## Inviting people

Open the event and choose **Invite attendees**. Search and tick as many people as you like; anyone already invited is skipped. Invitees who can sign in to SYNAPSE are notified. Employees without an account are still invited — they just aren't notified.

## Tracking replies

The event's tiles count **invited**, **accepted**, **tentative** and **not replied**. Set each person's reply in the attendees table: **Accepted**, **Tentative** or **Declined**. "Attending" counts accepted and tentative.

**Remind pending** sends another notification to everyone who hasn't replied — not once the event is over.

## Adding it to a calendar

**Add to calendar** downloads the event as a calendar file that Outlook, Google Calendar and Apple Calendar can open.

## Archiving

**Archive** an event to take it off the list; find it under the **Archived** status filter to restore it. An event with attendees can't be deleted permanently — archive it instead.

> [!NOTE]
> Each employee's profile has an **Events** tab listing what they're invited to and their reply.
