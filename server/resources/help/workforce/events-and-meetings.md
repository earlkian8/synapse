**Workforce** → **Events & Meetings** is your company calendar: town halls, trainings, team meetings and celebrations, with who is invited and who is coming. The buttons at the top right move between **All events**, **Rooms** and **My invitations** — you see the ones your role includes. Answering your own invitations is explained in [Answering your invitations](/help/workforce/answering-event-invitations).

## The events list

The tiles count upcoming events, events in all, meetings and invitations. The table lists each event with its kind and organiser, schedule, location, how many are attending out of those invited, and its status. Search, filter by kind and status, and sort. An event that repeats says so under its title.

An event's status comes from its times: **Upcoming** before it starts, **Ongoing** while it runs, and **Past** once it ends.

## Scheduling an event

Choose **New event** and give it a title, its kind — an **Event** or a **Meeting** — a description, and start and end times. Times are on **your company's clock**, so everyone sees the same time whatever their own time zone. You are recorded as the organiser.

- **Room** — pick one of your company's rooms; the list shows which are free at that time. A room another event already holds is refused, with the event that holds it. Or type a **Location** instead, such as an address or a video link.
- **Reminder** — invitees who haven't declined are reminded automatically, from 10 minutes to 2 days before it starts.
- **Repeats** — every day, week or month (every 1 to 4 of them; a weekly event on the weekdays you choose), ending **After** a number of times or **On a date**, up to two years ahead. Each date becomes its own event in the list; a room must be free on every one.

### Changing a repeating event

Editing, inviting to or archiving one date of a repeating event asks whether it's for **this event** or **this and the following events**. Moving the time of the following events keeps each on its own day.

## Inviting people

Open the event and choose **Invite attendees**. Search and tick as many people as you like; anyone already invited is skipped. Invitees who can sign in to SYNAPSE are notified, once even when they're invited to several dates. Employees without an account are still invited — they just aren't notified.

## Tracking replies

People answer their own invitations, or you can set a reply for them in the attendees table: **Accepted**, **Tentative** or **Declined**. The event's tiles count **invited**, **accepted**, **tentative** and **not replied**. "Attending" counts accepted and tentative.

**Remind pending** sends another notification to everyone who hasn't replied — not once the event is over.

## Rooms

**Rooms** shows each room and the events holding it, week by week; move between weeks with the arrows. A room is booked by scheduling an event in it. **New room** adds one with its location and capacity. Archive a room to stop new bookings — events that hold it keep it. A room no event ever used can be deleted for good; one that was booked can only be archived.

## Adding it to a calendar

**Add to calendar** downloads the event as a calendar file that Outlook, Google Calendar and Apple Calendar can open.

## Archiving

**Archive** an event to take it off the list; find it under the **Archived** status filter to restore it. An event with attendees can't be deleted permanently — archive it instead.

> [!NOTE]
> Each employee's profile has an **Events** tab listing what they're invited to and their reply.
