Your own time record lives at **[My Attendance](/attendance/me)**. You can clock in there from a computer, or from the SYNAPSE mobile app — both record to the same place. See [Clocking in from your phone](/help/mobile-app/clocking-in-on-mobile).

> [!TIP]
> People who review the team's attendance also find **My attendance** at the top of the **Attendance** page.

## The clock card

The card shows your company's current time and date, and **your current shift**. Its main button always offers the next thing to do:

**Clock in** → **Start break** → **End break** → **Clock out**

Press it at each point in your day. Your location is captured with every punch, and you can attach a selfie if you like (your company's policy may require one in the mobile app).

If you work nights, the card keeps showing the shift you started — at 2 a.m. you still see last night's shift, not an empty new day.

## Your record

Under the card:

- **Today's punches** — each punch so far;
- a summary of this month — days present, late, absent and hours;
- **Recent history** — your past days, with their status.

What each status means is explained in [Reviewing attendance](/help/workforce/reviewing-attendance) (if your role can open it) and in brief here: **Present**, **Late** (after your start time plus any grace), **Undertime** (left early or worked short), **Half day**, **Absent**, **On leave**, **Holiday**, **Day off**, and **Incomplete** (clocked in, not yet out).

## If a punch is refused

Your company's attendance policy decides what counts. A punch can be refused when:

- you are **outside every work site's fence** and the policy blocks punches from elsewhere — the message names the nearest site and how far away you are;
- the policy only accepts punches **from your company's network** and you aren't on it;
- the policy **doesn't accept punches from the web** (only from the app, say);
- the punch doesn't fit — clocking in twice, or clocking out before clocking in.

A refused punch records nothing. Ask your HR team to record the time for you.

## Forgot to clock in or out?

You can't change your own punches. Tell your HR team or manager, who can [correct the day](/help/workforce/correcting-and-signing-off-attendance). If you forget to clock out, your company's policy may close the day for you at the end of your shift and flag it for a manager to check.

> [!NOTE]
> Your company may send you a reminder if you haven't clocked in some time after your shift starts. It is sent once per shift, and never while you're on leave, on a holiday or on a rest day.
