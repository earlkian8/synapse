How you ask for time off depends on what your role includes:

- **Everyone** can file leave from the **SYNAPSE mobile app**: open **Leave**, file a request, and follow it there. See [Your leave, awards and profile in the app](/help/mobile-app/leave-on-mobile).
- **If your role includes Leave Management**, you can also use **File leave** on the [Managing leave](/help/workforce/managing-leave) screen.
- **If the assistant is offered to you**, you can ask it: "file vacation leave for me from the 12th to the 14th".

## Filing a request

1. Choose the **leave type**.
2. Choose the **dates**. For a single day, you can make it a **half day** if the leave type allows it.
3. Add a **reason**.
4. Submit.

You're shown how many working days the request will charge. Weekends and your company's non-working holidays aren't counted.

## What happens next

- If the leave type needs approval, the request waits as **pending** and your HR team is notified. You'll be notified when it is approved or rejected.
- If it doesn't need approval, it is approved straight away.

## Your balance

For each leave type you can see how many days you are **entitled** to this year, how many you have **used**, how many are **pending**, and how many **remain**.

## Changing your mind

A request that is still **pending** can be cancelled. Once it has been decided, ask your HR team.

> [!NOTE]
> On days covered by approved leave, your attendance shows **On leave** — you won't be marked absent.
