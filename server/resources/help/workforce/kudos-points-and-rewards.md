Everyone who takes part in recognition can thank colleagues, nominate them for awards, and spend the points they earn. Open **Workforce** → **Awards & Recognition** — or use the **SYNAPSE mobile app**, which has the same features.

## The recognition wall

**Wall** shows the kudos colleagues sent each other and the awards given in the last 90 days, newest first. Switch between **Everything**, **Kudos** and **Awards**, and choose **Show more** to go further back.

### Sending kudos

1. Under **Send kudos**, choose who you're thanking.
2. Say what they did — be specific; it's what they'll remember. A line is enough, up to 500 characters.
3. Choose **Send kudos**.

Your colleague is notified and your kudos appear on the wall. Each kudos gives them points, up to a monthly number your company sets; the card beside the wall shows how many you have left. Past that number, kudos still go out — without points. You can't send kudos to yourself.

## Nominating a colleague

Choose **Nominate a colleague** on the wall, or open **My nominations**. Pick the colleague and an award type that is open to nominations, and say why in at least 20 characters — if HR approves it, your reason becomes the citation.

**My nominations** lists everyone you've put forward and what was decided. A nomination still waiting can be withdrawn. You'll be notified when HR approves or turns it down.

## Your points

**My points** shows your balance, the rewards it can buy, your requests, and every line that explains your balance. Awards and kudos add points; a reward you redeem takes them away.

### Redeeming a reward

1. Choose **Redeem** on a reward you have enough points for.
2. Add a note for HR if it helps — a size, a date.
3. Confirm. The points are held straight away and the request waits for HR.

HR hands the reward over, or declines it and your points come back. A request still waiting can be cancelled, which also returns the points.

> [!NOTE]
> If your account isn't linked to an employee record, you can read the wall but not send kudos or earn points. Ask your HR team to link it.
