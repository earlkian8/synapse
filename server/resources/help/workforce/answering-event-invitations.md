When you're invited to an event or a meeting, you're notified and it appears under **Workforce** → **Events & Meetings** → **My invitations** — and in the **SYNAPSE mobile app**.

## Your invitations

**Coming up** lists what's ahead, day by day; **Past 30 days** shows the last month. Each invitation shows the time, the room or location, the organiser, how many are coming and any reminder.

A repeating event — a weekly stand-up, say — shows its next date only. Tick **Show every date of repeating events** to see them all.

## Answering

Choose **Going**, **Maybe** or **Not going**. Your answer goes straight to the organiser, and you can change it until the event ends.

For a repeating event, tick **Also for later dates** before answering to give the same answer for every later date.

## Reminders

If the organiser set a reminder, you're notified before the event starts — unless you answered **Not going**.

## Adding to your calendar

- **Add to calendar** downloads one event as a calendar file.
- **Subscribe** gives you a private link that Google Calendar, Outlook or Apple Calendar follows, so every invitation — and every change to one — appears there by itself. Calendar apps check it on their own schedule; Google Calendar can take several hours to show a change.

> [!WARNING]
> Anyone with your subscription link can see your invitations. If it's been shared, choose **Reset link** in the Subscribe window: the old link stops working, and you add the new one to your calendar.

Your calendar app shows your invitations; answering still happens in SYNAPSE.
