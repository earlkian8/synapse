When somebody on your team breaks a rule, report it to HR with the facts. Open **Workforce** → **Discipline** → **Reported by me**.

1. Choose **Report an incident**.
2. Choose the **Employee**, and the **Offense** if you know which.
3. Give the **Date**, the **Time** and **Where**.
4. Under **What happened**, write the facts in order, as you saw them. HR may put them on a notice to explain, so be specific.
5. Under **Who saw it**, name any witnesses. Only HR sees them.
6. Choose **Send to HR**.

Nothing is sent to the employee until HR serves a notice. Your report then shows how far it has got. What the employee answers and why a case was decided stay with HR.
