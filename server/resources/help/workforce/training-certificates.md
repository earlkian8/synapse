A program can issue a **certificate** to everyone who completes it. Certificates are numbered, dated, and tracked until they expire.

## Turning certificates on

Edit the program and turn on **Completing it earns a certificate**. Give **Valid for (months)** if it expires — 24 for two years — or leave it blank if it never does.

From then on, marking someone **completed** issues their certificate:

- it is dated on the day they completed, and expires that many months later;
- it gets a number, like _TC-2026-00042_;
- it appears on their **My training** and on their profile's **Certifications** tab.

Turning certificates on for a program that people already finished issues one to each of them. Changing a program later doesn't change certificates already issued.

If you mark someone back to enrolled or dropped, or take them off the roster, their certificate is withdrawn. It can't be removed from the profile: change the enrollment instead.

## The register

**Training & Development** → **Certificates** lists every certificate training has issued, with its standing:

| Standing          | Means                                                       |
| ----------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Valid**         | In date, or it never expires.                               |
| **Expiring soon** | It expires within 60 days.                                  |
| **Expired**       | Its expiry date has passed.                                 |
| **Renewed**       | The person has since earned a newer one of the same name.   |

Search by person, certificate or number, filter by standing, and sort. **Export** downloads the register.

## Expiry reminders

60 days before a certificate expires, its holder is told once, and so is whoever runs training. A renewed certificate is left alone.

## Renewing

From an expiring or expired certificate's menu, choose **Renew by enrolling again** and pick a program that's running or coming up. Completing it issues the new certificate, and the old one shows as **Renewed**.

## Printing

Open a certificate to see it laid out on a landscape page, then choose **Print or save as PDF**. Holders can open and print their own from **My training**.
