Different managers rate against different bars. A **calibration session** puts a cycle's submitted ratings side by side, so the people running the cycle can move any rating that reads differently from the rest before employees see their results.

## Opening a session

Go to **Workforce** → **Performance Management** → **Calibration**, pick the review cycle, and choose **New session**. Give the session:

- **a name**, for example _Sales and Operations, mid-year_;
- **what it covers**: everyone in the cycle, or the departments you choose;
- **when it meets** (optional), **who takes part**, and any **notes** about the bar to hold everyone to.

Two open sessions in one cycle can't cover the same people.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> While a session is open, the appraisals it covers are **held back**. They can still be submitted, but their employees can't read them until the session ends. Each employee sees **Being calibrated** instead.

## The session

The session page shows each submitted appraisal it covers with:

- the attainment;
- the rating the scorecard gave (**Scorecard gave**);
- the rating it has now (**Rated now**).

Above the list, the spread across your rating bands is shown **before** and **after** calibration. That makes a department that rates generously, or harshly, easy to see. Appraisals that are still being rated are listed but can't be moved yet.

## Moving a rating

Choose **Move** on an appraisal. Then pick the band it belongs in and say why. The reason is required, and it stays on the record with the move.

- Only the rating moves. The attainment stays as scored.
- Moving a rating back to what the scorecard gave clears the calibration.
- You can't move your own rating. Another calibrator has to.
- An acknowledged appraisal is final and can't be moved.

The appraisal shows every move made to it: from what, to what, by whom and why.

## Ending the session

- **Complete** closes the session. Its moves stand, and every appraisal it was holding back is shared with its employee, who is notified.
- **Cancel session** calls it off before anything has been moved. The appraisals it was holding back are shared as they stand.

A session that has moved a rating can only be completed, not cancelled.

See [Performance appraisals](/help/workforce/performance-appraisals) for rating and submitting the appraisals themselves.
