**Workforce** → **Training & Development** keeps your training programs and everyone enrolled in them, from enrollment to how each person finished. Its sections are **Programs**, **Schedule**, **Certificates** and **Budget**, plus **My training** and **Browse** for your own training.

## The programs list

The tiles count ongoing and upcoming programs, active enrollments and completions. The table lists each program with its provider, schedule, seats, completions and status. A program people can join themselves says so under its name, and so does one that earns a certificate. Search, filter by status, and sort. **Export** downloads the list.

A program's status comes from its dates — you never set it:

| Status        | When                                                  |
| ------------- | ----------------------------------------------------- |
| **Upcoming**  | Before its start date (or when it has no start date). |
| **Ongoing**   | From its start date.                                  |
| **Completed** | Once its end date has passed.                         |

## Creating a program

Choose **New program** and give it a name, provider, description, start and end dates, and — if seats are limited — a **capacity**. Leave capacity empty for no limit.

Then choose **who can join**:

- **HR enrolls people** — only you add people to the roster.
- **Anyone can join** — employees take a seat themselves under **Browse**, while seats last.
- **Anyone can ask to join** — employees ask, and you approve or decline each request.

Turn on **Completing it earns a certificate** to issue one to everyone you mark completed. See [Training certificates](/help/workforce/training-certificates). A **program budget** is what it may spend. See [Training budget and costs](/help/workforce/training-budget).

## Requests to join

Requests wait at the top of **Programs** and of the program's roster. **Approve** gives the person a seat, if one is left. **Decline** asks for an optional note. Either way, they're told. A request takes no seat until it's approved.

## A program's page

Open a program to see its header (provider, schedule, status, who can join, its certificate and budget) and six tiles: **seats taken**, **completion rate**, **average score**, **attendance**, **at risk** and **dropped**. Below them are three tabs.

### Roster

The roster lists everyone enrolled with their status, score, attendance, and enrolled and completed dates. A tick beside the status means they hold a certificate. Choose **Enroll** to add people — someone who asked to join is enrolled on their request. Once a program with a capacity is full, nobody else can be enrolled. Everyone you enroll is told.

Open someone's enrollment to record how it went:

- **Enrolled** — still taking part;
- **Completed** — finished, with a **score** from 0 to 100 and remarks. The completion date is set for you;
- **Dropped** — left before the end.

To update several people at once, tick them and mark them completed or dropped, or remove them.

Under the roster, an **AI insights** panel reads the program's results and suggests what to do about them.

### Sessions

When the program meets, and the attendance taken. See [Training sessions and attendance](/help/workforce/training-sessions-and-attendance).

### Costs

What the program has spent against its budget. See [Training budget and costs](/help/workforce/training-budget).

## Archiving

**Archive** a program you no longer run. Find it again under the **Archived** status filter, where you can restore it — or delete it permanently, unless people were enrolled in it.

> [!NOTE]
> Each employee's profile has a **Training** tab listing their programs. It is read-only: enrollments are managed here.
