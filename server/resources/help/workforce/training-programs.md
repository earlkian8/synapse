**Workforce** → **Training & Development** keeps your training programs and everyone enrolled in them, from enrollment to how each person finished.

## The programs list

The tiles count ongoing and upcoming programs, active enrollments and completions. The table lists each program with its provider, schedule, seats, completions and status. Search, filter by status, and sort. **Export** downloads the list.

A program's status comes from its dates — you never set it:

| Status        | When                                                  |
| ------------- | ----------------------------------------------------- |
| **Upcoming**  | Before its start date (or when it has no start date). |
| **Ongoing**   | From its start date.                                  |
| **Completed** | Once its end date has passed.                         |

## Creating a program

Choose **New program** and give it a name, provider, description, start and end dates, and — if seats are limited — a **capacity**. Leave capacity empty for no limit.

## A program's roster

Open a program to see its header (provider, schedule, status, description) and five tiles: **seats taken**, **completion rate**, **average score**, **at risk** and **dropped**.

The roster lists everyone enrolled with their status, score, and enrolled and completed dates. Choose **Enroll** to add people. Once a program with a capacity is full, nobody else can be enrolled.

Open someone's enrollment to record how it went:

- **Enrolled** — still taking part;
- **Completed** — finished, with a **score** from 0 to 100 and remarks. The completion date is set for you;
- **Dropped** — left before the end.

To update several people at once, tick them and mark them completed or dropped, or remove them.

Under the roster, an **AI insights** panel reads the program's results and suggests what to do about them.

## Archiving

**Archive** a program you no longer run. Find it again under the **Archived** status filter, where you can restore it — or delete it permanently, unless people were enrolled in it.

> [!NOTE]
> Each employee's profile has a **Training** tab listing their programs. It is read-only: enrollments are managed here.
