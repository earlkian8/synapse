**Workforce** → **Training & Development** is where you follow your own training. **My training** is yours; **Browse** lists what you can join.

## Joining a program

**Browse** shows the programs open to sign-ups. Each says when it runs, how many sessions it has, the seats left, and whether completing it earns a certificate.

- **Join** — you take a seat at once, while seats last.
- **Ask to join** — HR approves or declines your request, and you're told either way. If they decline, their note shows on the program.

HR can also enroll you in any program. You're told when they do.

## My training

**My training** has three tabs.

### Coming up

Your sessions, day by day: when, where, and any notes. **Add to calendar** downloads one session. To have every session appear in your calendar app by itself, choose **Subscribe** — the same link as your event invitations.

### Programs

Each of your programs, with your **attendance**, **score** and when you completed it.

- A request waiting for HR says so. **Withdraw request** takes it back.
- Until a program starts, and before any attendance is taken, **Leave program** gives up your seat. After that, ask HR.

### Certificates

The certificates you've earned, with their number and when each expires. You're told 60 days before one does. Open a certificate to print it or save it as a PDF. Your certificates are also on your profile.
