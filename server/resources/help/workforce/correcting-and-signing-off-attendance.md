People forget to clock out, phones die, and some days need a manager's approval before they count. Fixing and approving days needs the **manual entry, corrections & approvals** permission for attendance.

## Recording or correcting a day

1. Open the person's day from **Attendance** (or use **Add punches** / **Edit punches** in its row's **⋯** menu).
2. Choose **Record** for a day with nothing on it, or **Correct** for one that has punches.
3. Enter the clock-in, break start, break end and clock-out — in the order they happen. A running total shows what they add up to, for example "8h 30m worked after a 45m break".
4. Save. Lateness, overtime and the status are worked out for you from the person's schedule and policy.

Times are on your company's clock for that work date. A time earlier than the one before it means the next morning — so a night shift is entered as in **22:00**, out **06:00**.

The punches a correction replaces aren't lost: the day keeps them, struck through, so you can always see what it said before. Every correction is recorded in the [activity logs](/help/administration/activity-logs).

## Signing a day off

Some days wait for a manager before they count fully. A day needs a sign-off when:

- it has **overtime that needs approval** under its policy;
- someone **punched away from the site** and the policy flags that;
- a punch came from a **source the policy doesn't allow**;
- a phone's **clock was off** by more than the policy allows;
- a forgotten clock-out was **closed automatically**.

Open the day and choose **Sign off**. Signing off grants the day's overtime as it stands; if a later correction adds more, the day goes back to waiting.

To approve everything at once, use **Sign off N days** at the top of the page. Nobody can sign off their own day — those are left for someone else.

## Re-applying the rules

A day is judged by the schedule, attendance policy and holidays that applied when it opened, and stays that way. When a past day should follow a change — a schedule fixed after the fact, a holiday added late — re-apply the rules:

- **one day:** open it and choose **Re-apply rules**;
- **a period:** choose **Re-apply rules** at the top of the page. It re-judges every recorded day in the day, week or month on screen (narrowed to the department you've filtered to) by each person's **current** schedule, policy and the holiday calendar.

> [!WARNING]
> Re-applying can change statuses and totals across many days at once — and with them any overtime and payroll figures. Check the period and department before you confirm.

## Deleting a day

Open the day and use the delete button at the bottom. This removes the record and its punches; if the person was due at work, the day will read as absent.

## When a punch was refused

If an employee tells you SYNAPSE refused their punch — they were outside the site under a blocking policy, or their phone sent it too late — record the day for them here, and add a remark saying why.
