Training money is kept in two places: what each **program** costs, and the company's training **budget** for each year. Spend is never typed in — it adds up the costs you record.

## Recording costs

Open a program and choose the **Costs** tab, then **Record a cost**. Give:

- what it was **for**: trainer fee, venue, materials, travel, meals, certification or other;
- the **amount**, in pesos;
- the day it was **incurred** — this decides which year's budget it counts against;
- optional details.

The tab shows the program's **budget** against its spend, spend **per participant** and **per completion**, and where the money went. Edit or remove a cost from its menu.

Set a program's own budget when you create or edit it.

## The year's budget

**Training & Development** → **Budget** shows one year at a time. Choose **Set the budget** for the year (**Change budget** once it has one) to give its training budget, with a note if you like. Then:

- the tiles show the budget, what was **spent**, what **remains** (or how far **over budget** it is), and what was **given to programs** that start that year;
- **Spend by category** and **spend by month** show where and when the money went;
- **Programs** lists each program that spent that year or starts in it, with its own budget against its spend;
- **Costs** lists every cost recorded that year.

Costs on an archived program still count: the money was spent.

> [!NOTE]
> Employees never see costs or budgets. **My training** and **Browse** show only the training itself.
