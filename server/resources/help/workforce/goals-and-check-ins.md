A **goal** is something to achieve during a review cycle, for example _Reduce ticket backlog to 20_ or _Complete a certification_. People check in on their goals as the cycle goes, so progress is visible long before the appraisal.

## How a goal is measured

Each goal is measured in one of two ways:

- **Progress to 100%**: you say how far along it is.
- **A number to reach**: it moves from a start toward a target, in a unit. For example, a backlog going from 120 tickets down to 20 tickets.

Each goal has a **weight**. A goal weighted 2 counts twice as much as one weighted 1. Each goal also has a **health**:

- **On track**
- **At risk**
- **Off track**

## My goals

**Workforce** → **Performance Management** → **My goals** shows your goals for one review cycle. You can pick another cycle from the picker at the top.

- Goals that HR or your manager set for you appear here, and you're notified when one is set.
- **Add a goal** lets you add one of your own, once the cycle is open.
- Open a goal and choose **Check in** to record where it stands now. Give the current value, its health and a note about what changed.

You can delete a goal you added yourself, as long as nobody has checked in on it yet.

## Goals (for HR)

**Workforce** → **Performance Management** → **Goals** shows every goal in the cycle. Above the list are counts of how many goals are on track, at risk, off track or stale. You can search the list and filter it by state or by department.

- **Set a goal** gives one goal to one person or to several people at once.
    - Start from the **goal library** to copy its wording and target.
    - Or write the goal from scratch.
- Open a goal to check in on it, edit it, or close it:
    - **Mark achieved**: it counts in full.
    - **Mark missed**: it counts at the progress it reached.
    - **Drop — it no longer counts**: it no longer counts. Its check-ins are kept.
    - **Reopen**: it becomes active again.
- **Delete** is only for a goal set by mistake that nobody has checked in on yet. Drop a goal with history instead.

> [!NOTE]
> A goal that nobody has checked in on for 30 days is marked **stale**, so it stands out on the list.

## Goals on the appraisal

Each person's goals for the cycle appear on their appraisal, with their **goal attainment**. Attainment is the weighted average of their goals' progress:

- An achieved goal counts as 100%.
- A dropped goal is left out.

Attainment informs the rating. It doesn't set it: the evaluator still rates the scorecard.

When a cycle is closed, its goals are final. They can't be checked in on or changed.

## The goal library

**Company Setup** → **Performance Framework** → **Goal library** holds the goals your company sets often, each with its wording and target. Setting a goal from the library copies the entry, so editing the library later never changes a goal that's already been set. See [Performance framework](/help/company-setup/performance-framework).
