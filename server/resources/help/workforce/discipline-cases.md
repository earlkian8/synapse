SYNAPSE takes every disciplinary case through the **twin-notice rule** of the Labor Code and DOLE Department Order No. 147-15: a written notice to explain, a real chance to answer, a hearing when one is asked for, and a written decision. Open **Workforce** → **Discipline**.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> The system won't let a case skip a step. There's no decision without a notice to explain, none before the employee could answer, none while a hearing they asked for is still to be held, and no dismissal on a company rule alone.

## 1. Read the report

A supervisor or HR reports what happened: who, when, where, what, and who saw it. The employee hears nothing yet. Read it under **Cases** → **Open**. If it shouldn't go further, **Withdraw** it.

## 2. Serve a notice to explain

1. Choose **Serve a notice to explain**.
2. Choose the **Offense** from your code of conduct.
3. Write **The charge**: the specific rule, and how it was broken. A general description won't do.
4. Choose when the answer is **due**: at least five calendar days away.
5. Choose **Serve the notice**.

The employee is told at once and reads it under **My notices**. The report's narrative goes on the notice as the facts; the witnesses and the evidence stay with you. **Notice to explain** at the top of the case prints it, with a line for them to sign.

## 3. Their answer, and a hearing

They answer in writing in the app, or hand in a letter — choose **Record a paper explanation** to type it in. If they ask for a hearing, **Set a hearing**, hold it, then **Record the hearing**: the minutes, and whether they came. A hearing they don't attend still counts as their chance to be heard.

If the due date passes without an answer, their right to answer in writing is waived and you can decide on the evidence.

## Preventive suspension

When their presence is a serious and imminent threat to life, property or the evidence, place them on **Preventive suspension** while the case is heard. It runs at most **30 days** and isn't a penalty.

## 4. Decide

1. Choose **Decide the case**.
2. Choose whether the charge is **upheld**.
3. Choose the **Penalty**. The code of conduct's step for how many times this has happened is offered first.
4. Write the **Reasons**: what the evidence showed, and why their explanation did or didn't answer it.
5. Choose **Serve the decision**.

The employee is told and acknowledges it. **Notice of decision** prints it. After a dismissal, start their exit in **Offboarding**.

## The code of conduct

Under **Code of conduct**, each offense has its gravity, the Labor Code ground it rests on — the just causes of Article 297, or a company rule — and a ladder of penalties for each repeat. A decision counts towards the next for the offense's period (12 months unless you set another). **Add the standard offenses** starts you with the ones most handbooks list, to edit to yours.

## Conduct records

Open an employee from a case to see their **conduct record**: where they stand today — in good standing, on a warning, serving a suspension — and the decisions that still count against them.
