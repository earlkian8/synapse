**Workforce** → **Employees** is your company's directory and the home of every employee's **201 file**: their personal and employment details, placement, documents, certifications and career history. Almost everything else in SYNAPSE — attendance, leave, appraisals, training — hangs off these records.

## The directory

The tiles at the top count total, active, regular, probationary and on-leave employees, and new hires this month. Below them, search by name, employee number or email; filter by department, status and employment type; and sort by any column. **Export** downloads the list with your current filters.

Click a row to open the employee. The **⋯** menu on each row has everything else.

## Adding an employee

Most people should arrive through a hire in [Recruitment](/help/talent-acquisition/interviews-and-hiring), which creates their record for you. To add someone directly — for example when bringing existing staff into SYNAPSE — choose **New employee** and fill in:

- **Personal information** — name and contact details.
- **Employment** — department, position, manager, employment type, status and key dates.
- **Compensation** — salary and payout details.
- **Government IDs** — statutory numbers used for filing.
- **System account** — optionally, the login this person signs in with.

Leave the employee number blank and SYNAPSE gives the next one (for example `EMP-00042`).

> [!NOTE]
> Adding an employee does not let them sign in. Invite them, or give them your join code — see [Inviting your people](/help/getting-started/inviting-your-people).

## The employee profile

Opening an employee shows their profile in tabs:

| Tab                | What it holds                                                                                    |
| ------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Profile**        | Their details. Government IDs and bank details are masked until you choose to reveal them.       |
| **Performance**    | Their appraisals.                                                                                |
| **Training**       | Programs they are enrolled in, and how each ended.                                               |
| **Awards**         | Recognitions they have received.                                                                 |
| **Events**         | Events they are invited to, and their replies.                                                   |
| **Offboarding**    | Their exit, if there is one.                                                                     |
| **Documents**      | Their 201 file: contracts, CV, government IDs and other documents. Upload and remove files here. |
| **Certifications** | Licences and certificates, with issue and expiry dates.                                          |
| **History**        | Their career timeline. A step is added automatically whenever their position or salary changes.  |

The Training, Awards, Events and Offboarding tabs are summaries — those records are managed in their own modules.

## Employment status

An employee is **Active**, **On leave**, **Suspended**, **Resigned** or **Terminated**. Change it quickly from the row's **⋯** menu. An exit run through [Offboarding](/help/offboarding/offboarding-an-employee) sets _Resigned_ or _Terminated_ for you when it is completed.

## Archiving, restoring and deleting

- **Archive** takes someone off the directory but keeps everything. Find archived employees with the **Archived** status filter, or in the [Trash bin](/help/administration/trash-bin), and **restore** them from there.
- **Delete permanently** removes an archived employee and their records for good — attendance, leave, appraisals and documents included. It cannot be undone.

## Working on many at once

Tick the boxes beside several employees to archive, restore, delete or change the status of all of them at once.

## Who can do what

Viewing, adding, editing, archiving, restoring, deleting permanently, exporting, managing documents and inviting are separate permissions. If a button is missing for you, your role doesn't include it.
