**Workforce** → **Check-ins** → **Team results** shows how the company and each team felt in a month, from the monthly check-ins, and how the last year has gone.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Results are for groups, never people. A group is shown only with **five or more answers**. You can't see who answered, what anyone answered, or anyone's notes, and no one else can either: not through the assistant, reports or a data export.

## Reading a month

Choose a month at the top. The tiles show:

| Tile | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| **Answered** | How many active employees answered, and the share. |
| **Mood** | The average mood, 1 (rough) to 5 (great). |
| **Engagement** | The three-item Utrecht Work Engagement Scale, 0 (never) to 6 (every day). |
| **Often think of leaving** | The share who answered *often* or *always* to "How often have you considered leaving your job?". |

**Mood over the year** shows how everyone's answers split across the five moods, month by month. An outlined month had fewer than five answers. Point at a month for its numbers. Beside it, small charts follow **job satisfaction**, **engagement** and **often think of leaving**, each on its own scale.

## By team

Teams are listed by name, never ranked. Each row shows how many answered, how their moods split, and their averages.

A team with fewer than five answers isn't listed alone. It's pooled with the other small teams into **Other teams**. If even that pool has fewer than five, the next-smallest team joins it, so a hidden team can never be worked out by subtracting the others from the company total.

## Turning check-ins on or off

With permission to manage check-ins, use the **Monthly check-ins** switch at the top. When they're off, nobody is asked or reminded, and the results already collected stay.

## How results reach the attrition model

Each team's latest shown result, never anyone's own answers, is stored with every attrition risk score. The general model can't read it, since the survey it learned from never asked it. Once your company trains a model on its own records ([Training a model on your own records](/help/analytics/model-graduation)), that model tries team mood with and without it, and keeps it only if it makes the score more accurate.
