**Workforce** → **Leave Management** is where leave requests are decided and balances are kept. It has two tabs: **Requests** and **Balances**.

## The requests inbox

The inbox opens on **Pending** — the requests waiting for a decision. Switch the status filter to see **Approved**, **Upcoming**, **Rejected**, **Cancelled** or **All statuses**, and narrow it by search, leave type and department.

Each row shows the employee, the leave type, the dates, the days charged, the status and when it was filed. A pending row has **Approve** and **Reject** right on it.

## Reviewing a request

Click a request to open it. The window is laid out the way a decision is made:

1. **Who and what** — the employee, the leave type, the dates and the days it will charge.
2. **The balance impact** — how much of this leave type they have for the year, and what would be left.
3. **The reason they gave**, and your **review note**.

Approve or reject it, with a note if you like. The employee is notified of your decision. From the same window you can also **edit** the request, **cancel** it, or delete it.

## How days are counted

A request charges **working days**: weekdays that aren't a non-working holiday on your [holiday calendar](/help/company-setup/work-schedules-and-holidays). A three-day request over a weekend charges three days, not five. A single day can be a **half day** when the leave type allows it.

Days already approved count as **used**; days in pending requests count as **pending**. Both come off the balance shown to the approver.

## Filing leave for someone

Choose **File leave**, pick the employee and leave type, the dates (and half day, if it is one), and add the reason. The form estimates the working days it will charge as you go.

A request of a leave type that doesn't need approval is approved straight away. Otherwise it waits in **Pending**, and your HR team is notified.

## Balances

The **Balances** tab shows every employee's leave for a year: one column per leave type, with what remains of what they're entitled to.

Each leave type has a default number of days per year (see [Leave types](/help/company-setup/leave-types)). To give someone a different entitlement — more days for long service, or a pro-rated first year — choose **Adjust** on their row and set the days for each type.

> [!NOTE]
> Only the entitlement is stored. Used and pending days are always worked out from the requests themselves, so a balance can never drift from what was actually taken.

## Who can do what

Viewing requests and balances, filing and cancelling requests, and approving, rejecting and setting balances are separate permissions. A Department Head, for example, can approve leave.
