Appraisals in SYNAPSE are scored against your own **performance framework** — your sections and criteria, each rated on its own scale, with the result told in your company's own words (for example _Exceeds expectations_) rather than a fixed 1–5. Setting the framework up is covered in [Performance framework](/help/company-setup/performance-framework).

## The cycle overview

**Workforce** → **Performance Management** shows one **review cycle** at a time — the open one by default; pick another from the cycle picker at the top. You'll see:

- **coverage** against your active headcount, how many appraisals are in progress, how many await sign-off, and the average result;
- the **result spread** across your rating bands;
- **department calibration** — how far each department's average sits from the cycle's, so an unusually generous or harsh department stands out;
- the **appraisals** themselves: search, filter by status, and sort by employee, framework, result or status. **Export** downloads the cycle.

## Starting appraisals

- **Launch cycle** opens appraisals for everyone active, or for the active staff of the departments you choose. Each person gets the framework that covers them — the most specific one for their position, then department, then employment type, then everyone — unless you choose one for the whole launch. Anyone already appraised in the cycle is skipped, so it is safe to run again as people join. You're told who was opened and who was left out, and why.
- **Open appraisal** starts a single one.

Appraisals can only be opened while a cycle is **open**.

## Rating a scorecard

Open an appraisal to see its scorecard. Each weighted **section** is a heading with its weight and its result so far; under it, each **criterion** with its weight within the section, a rating and room for evidence.

Rate each criterion on its own scale — choosing a named level shows what that level means, and a percentage target has a slider. The result updates as you go, and the **rating ladder** shows where it stands among your bands. **Save** keeps your work; **Delete draft** discards it.

### How the result is worked out

1. Each rating becomes a position on its own scale.
2. Ratings are weighted **within** their section to give the section's result.
3. Sections are weighted **against each other** to give the appraisal's result, from 0 to 100.
4. That number is read against your rating bands to give the label.

Only rated criteria count, so a draft always has a running result; a section with nothing rated yet is left out rather than pulling the result down.

## Submitting and signing off

| Status                | Meaning                             |
| --------------------- | ----------------------------------- |
| **In progress**       | A draft, still being rated.         |
| **Awaiting sign-off** | Submitted. The scorecard is locked. |
| **Signed off**        | Final.                              |

**Submit** becomes available once every criterion is rated. Then **Record sign-off** to make it final.

> [!NOTE]
> An appraisal keeps the framework, scales and bands it was opened under. Changing your framework later affects the next appraisals, never past ones.

## Decision support

Above the scorecard, **decision support** shows the person's rating trajectory across past appraisals, their strengths and gaps, and — where there is one — the latest forecast of their next result. Below the scorecard, **AI Coaching Insights** asks an AI model to read the appraisal and suggest how to support the person; **Regenerate** asks again.

Both inform the conversation. Neither changes the rating — that is always yours.
