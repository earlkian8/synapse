A **notice to explain** says what you're charged with and asks for your side in writing. You have at least five calendar days to answer. Open **Workforce** → **Discipline** → **My notices**; you're told when one arrives.

## Reading it

The notice gives **the charge** — the rule and how it is said to have been broken — and what is reported to have happened. **Notice to explain** at the top prints it.

Choose **I've received it** so HR knows it reached you. It doesn't mean you agree.

## Answering

1. Under **Your explanation**, write what happened from your side, and anything that explains or excuses it.
2. To explain in person, tick **I ask for a hearing**. HR has to hold it before deciding. You may bring a representative, such as a lawyer or a union officer.
3. Choose **Send my explanation**.

You can change it until the case is decided. An answer sent after the due date is still read, but don't count on it: once the date passes, HR may decide without it.

## The decision

When HR decides, you're told. The decision says whether the charge was upheld, the penalty if any, and why. Choose **I've received it** once you've read it. **Notice of decision** prints it.

> [!NOTE]
> While a case is heard, HR may place you on preventive suspension for up to 30 days. It isn't a penalty.
