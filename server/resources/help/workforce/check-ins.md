Once a month, SYNAPSE asks six quick questions about how work is going. It takes about a minute. Open **Workforce** → **Check-ins**, or use the **SYNAPSE mobile app**, where a card on Home tells you when this month's is waiting.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Your answers are confidential. No one at your company sees them, not HR, not your manager, not the assistant. HR sees results only for groups of five or more people, and smaller groups are pooled until they're big enough.

## Answering this month's

1. Choose how you've felt at work this month, from **Rough** to **Great**. The face beside the questions takes on the mood you pick.
2. Say how satisfied you are with your job overall.
3. Three statements about energy, enthusiasm and focus: choose how often each was true this month, from **Never** to **Always** (every day).
4. Say how often you've considered leaving your job. Answer honestly: it's one of the most useful things your company can learn, and no one can trace it to you.
5. Read your answers back. Choose **Change** beside any you want to redo.
6. Add a note for yourself if you like (what made the month what it was). **Only you can read it.**
7. Choose **Save check-in**.

You can answer with the keyboard too: number keys pick an answer.

## Changing your answers

Until the month ends, open **My check-in** and choose **Change my answers**. Saving again replaces that month's answers; you can't add a second one. Past months can't be changed.

## Looking back

**Your year** shows your last twelve months as twelve faces. A month you didn't answer is an outline. Choose a month to see what you answered then, and your note.

## Reminders

You're told on the 1st of each month that the check-in is open, and once more on the 20th if you haven't answered yet. If your company turns check-ins off, nothing is asked.

## What the questions are

They're published, tested measures rather than questions written for the app: a single question on overall job satisfaction, the three-item Utrecht Work Engagement Scale, and the first question of the Turnover Intention Scale ("How often have you considered leaving your job?").
