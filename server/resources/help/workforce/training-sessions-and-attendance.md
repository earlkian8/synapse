A program meets in **sessions**: a day, a start and an end time, and where. Attendance is taken at each one, so you can see who actually came.

## Scheduling sessions

Open a program and choose the **Sessions** tab, then **Schedule session**. Give it:

- a **day** inside the program's dates;
- a **start** and an **end** time, on your company's clock;
- optionally a **title** (_Day 1: Foundations_), **where** it is, and notes.

Turn on **Repeat daily** to add one session per day up to a date, on weekdays only if you like. A five-day course is one entry.

A program's dates can't move off a session it already has. Move or cancel the session first.

## Taking attendance

From the day of a session, open it (or choose **Take attendance** from its menu). Mark each person who holds a seat:

| Mark        | Counts as                                     |
| ----------- | --------------------------------------------- |
| **Present** | Attended.                                     |
| **Late**    | Attended.                                     |
| **Absent**  | Not attended.                                 |
| **Excused** | Left out of their rate — it doesn't count.    |

**Mark the rest present** fills in everyone you haven't marked. Press a mark again to clear it.

Each person's **attendance** is the sessions they attended out of the sessions counted for them. It shows on the roster, on the program's tiles, and on their **My training**.

> [!NOTE]
> Cancelling a session removes the attendance taken at it, and everyone's rate is worked out without it.

## The schedule

**Training & Development** → **Schedule** shows every session of every program in a month. Use the arrows to move between months, and filter to one program. A session with a small amber dot was held, but nobody took attendance. Programs running that month with no sessions yet are listed underneath.

Each person also sees their own sessions on **My training**, and they appear in the calendar they subscribed to from **My invitations**.
