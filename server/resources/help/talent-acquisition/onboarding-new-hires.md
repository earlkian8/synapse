Onboarding turns a hire into a productive colleague. Each new employee gets an **onboarding checklist** — paperwork, equipment, access, orientation, training and compliance tasks — with an owner and a due date for each, built from one of your [onboarding programs](/help/company-setup/onboarding-programs).

## How onboarding starts

- **Automatically**, when someone is hired through Recruitment. SYNAPSE picks the program that best matches them: one for their department and employment type, then their department, then their employment type, then your default program.
- **By hand**, for anyone added another way: choose **Start onboarding**, pick the employee (only active employees without a checklist are offered) and, optionally, the program.

Each employee has at most one onboarding.

## The three levels

**Talent Acquisition** → **Onboarding** reads from the top down:

1. **Programs** — one row per program: who it applies to, its number of tasks, how many people it is onboarding and has finished, their progress and overdue tasks. People onboarded without a program appear under **Unassigned**.
2. **A program's people** — open a program to see everyone on it: progress, overdue tasks, start and target dates (a target that has passed shows in red). Filter by department and status.
3. **A person's checklist** — open someone to work through their tasks.

## Working through a checklist

Tasks are grouped by category, each with its done count. For each task you can see its owner, its due date (red when overdue), and who completed it and when.

- Tick a task to mark it **done**.
- From its **⋯** menu, mark it **in progress**, **skip** it (or un-skip it), edit it or delete it.
- Add a one-off task the program doesn't have.
- Edit the onboarding's notes and target date.

Assigning a task to someone notifies them. The checklist starts as **pending** and moves to **in progress** by itself the first time a task is touched. Progress counts tasks done or skipped.

## Finishing, cancelling and reopening

- **Complete** the onboarding when it's done. You can complete it with tasks still open — sometimes that is right — and SYNAPSE records how many were left.
- **Cancel** it if the hire didn't go ahead (you'll be asked to confirm).
- **Reopen** a completed or cancelled onboarding to carry on.

> [!TIP]
> If the assistant is offered to you, ask it to "chase everything overdue on Maria's checklist". It sends each owner one reminder listing all of their outstanding tasks, rather than one message per task.

## Who can do what

Viewing onboarding, running checklists (starting, ticking, completing) and managing programs are separate permissions.
