## Scheduling an interview

1. Open the candidate from the posting's pipeline.
2. Under **Interviews**, choose **Schedule**.
3. Pick when, the mode (**On-site**, **Online** or **Phone**), the interviewer, and where — an office, or a meeting link.
4. Choose **Schedule interview**.

Scheduling an interview moves the candidate on to the next stage of your pipeline, so the board keeps up without an extra step. Upcoming interviews also appear in the queue on the dashboard of whoever can act on them.

## Recording the outcome

After the interview, set its result to **Passed** or **Failed** from the candidate's interview list. The result feeds the candidate's [fit score](/help/talent-acquisition/candidates-and-the-pipeline), and a failed interview makes **reject** the recommended next step.

## Hiring

When you have chosen someone, open them and choose **Hire**. In one step SYNAPSE:

1. **creates their employee record** from the candidate's name and contact details and the posting's department, position and employment type — active from today, with the next employee number;
2. **copies their résumé** into their 201 file;
3. **marks the application hired**, and the posting **filled** once every opening has been hired;
4. **starts their onboarding** on the program that best matches their department and employment type — see [Onboarding new hires](/help/talent-acquisition/onboarding-new-hires).

Your HR team is notified of the hire.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Hiring is the only way a candidate reaches the **Hired** stage — moving them there by hand isn't possible. That way every hire has an employee record behind it.

## After the hire

The new employee can't sign in yet: hiring creates their employment, not an account. Invite them from **Employees** → **App access**, or give them your join code — see [Inviting your people](/help/getting-started/inviting-your-people). Then check their record in [Employee records](/help/workforce/employee-records) and complete anything the posting didn't cover, such as their manager and schedule.

## Rejecting

Choose **Reject** on a candidate (from their profile, or the **⋯** menu on the table or board), and add a reason if you like. They leave the active pipeline, but their record and history stay — and if you change your mind, **Move to…** puts them back on any stage.
