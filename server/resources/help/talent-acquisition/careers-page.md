Your company has a public **careers page** that lists every open posting, and each open posting has its own page with an application form. Nobody needs an account to apply.

## Sharing a posting

On **Recruitment**, open a posting's **⋯** menu and choose **Copy public link**. Share it wherever you advertise — job boards, social media, an email. **View details** shows the same link.

Only **open** postings are public. A draft, closed or filled posting's page can't be reached, and a posting past its closing date stops taking applications even before it is closed.

## What applicants see

Each posting shows where the role is (for example "Hybrid · Makati HQ", with the site's address on its page), the employment type, the minimum experience and the closing date. When you've given a pay range and left **Show the pay on the careers page** on, it shows the pay as well, for example "₱25,000–₱35,000 a month".

## What applicants are asked

The application form asks for:

- their name and contact details;
- their years of experience (half years such as 2.5 are fine);
- a **résumé** — unless the posting turned **Ask for a résumé** off;
- their **expected pay**, in the posting's period (for example "Expected monthly pay") — unless the posting turned **Ask for expected pay** off;
- optional supporting documents, such as a cover letter, certificates or a portfolio;
- answers to the posting's own **screening questions**, as Yes or No.

Résumés and documents can be PDF, Word or image files of up to 10 MB each.

## What happens next

Each application appears on the posting's pipeline straight away, in the first stage, ranked by fit (unless ranking is off), and your HR team is notified. Its source is recorded as your website.

Someone who applies again with the same email address updates their existing candidate record rather than creating a duplicate — and a second application to the same posting is ignored.

> [!NOTE]
> The form is protected against spam: it limits how often one visitor can submit, and quietly drops automated submissions.
