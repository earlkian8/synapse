A **job posting** is a vacancy you are hiring for. It belongs to a department, runs on one of your **hiring pipelines** (the stages its candidates move through), and — once published — has its own public page where anyone can apply.

## The postings list

**Talent Acquisition** → **Recruitment** lists every posting in one table: its linked position, department, type, openings (hired out of seats), how many candidates are active, its status, and its closing date. Search, or filter by department and status. Click a row to open its **pipeline**.

The **⋯** menu on a row has **View details**, **Open pipeline**, **Copy public link**, **Edit**, **Set status** and **Delete**. **Export** downloads the list with your current filters.

## Creating a posting

1. Choose **New posting**.
2. Fill in the **Job title**, **Department**, **Position**, **Employment type**, the number of **Openings**, and the **Description** and **Requirements** applicants will read.
3. Choose the **pipeline** it runs on. New postings start on your default pipeline; see [Recruitment pipelines](/help/company-setup/recruitment-pipelines).
4. Decide two things:
    - **Require a résumé** — on by default. Turn it off for roles where a CV isn't how people apply.
    - **Rank candidates automatically** — on by default. Turn it off and candidates are simply listed by when they applied.
5. Optionally add **screening criteria**: **Minimum experience (years)**, **Required skills**, and your own **Screening questions** that applicants answer Yes or No (for example "Do you hold a valid driver's licence?").
6. Choose a **Status** — **Draft** to keep working on it, or **Open** to publish it — and a **Closing date**, which publishing requires. Save.

## Statuses

| Status     | Meaning                                                                                             |
| ---------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Draft**  | Being prepared. Not public.                                                                         |
| **Open**   | Published: it has a public page and accepts applications. An open posting must have a closing date. |
| **Closed** | No longer accepting applications.                                                                   |
| **Filled** | Every opening has been hired.                                                                       |

A posting fills itself when its last opening is hired, and closes itself the day after its closing date passes. The pipeline shows a countdown to the closing date, and **Expired** once it has passed. Change the status yourself with **Set status**.

> [!TIP]
> Copy a posting's public link from its row's **⋯** menu to share it on job boards and social media. See [Your public careers page](/help/talent-acquisition/careers-page).

## What you can do depends on your role

Viewing recruitment, creating and editing postings, moving candidates, scheduling interviews, hiring and exporting are separate permissions, so a role can, for example, review candidates without being able to hire. If an action is missing for you, your role doesn't include it.
