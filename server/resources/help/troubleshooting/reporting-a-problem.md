When something in SYNAPSE is broken, slow or looks wrong, tell the SYNAPSE team — the people who build and run the system. Every report is read, and you can follow yours until it's settled.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> A mistake in your own records — a leave balance, a time entry, your contact details — is for your HR team to correct, not the SYNAPSE team. Ask them directly.

## Report a problem from the web

1. Open the page where the problem happens.
2. Choose **Help** (the question mark in the top bar) → **Report a problem**.
3. Pick what kind of problem it is: **Something isn't working**, **Something looks wrong**, **It's slow or won't load**, or **Something else**.
4. Give it a short **Summary**, and under **What happened?** say what you were doing, what you expected, and what happened instead. Copy in any error message you saw.
5. Say how much it's getting in your way.
6. Add up to three **Screenshots** if they help. You can choose a file, drop one on the box, or paste one you've just taken with **Ctrl+V** (**⌘V** on a Mac).
7. Choose **Send report**.

You'll see the report's reference, such as **PR-7K2M9Q**. Quote it if you ever talk to someone about the problem.

You can also report a problem from the bottom of any Help Center page, under **Still need help?**

> [!TIP]
> Report from the page that has the problem. The report records which page it was sent from, which helps the team find it quickly.

## Report a problem from the app

1. Go to **Profile** → **Report a problem**.
2. Pick the kind of problem, choose where in the app it happens, and describe it.
3. Add screenshots from your photos if they help, then tap **Send**.

## What is sent with your report

Besides what you write, the report carries your name and email (so the team can reply), the workspace you were in, the page or part of the app, your browser or phone's system and the app's version, and a few display facts: the window size, your time zone, your language and light or dark mode. In the web dialog, open **Sent with your report** to see exactly what goes.

Nobody in your company reads your report. Your workspace's activity log only notes that a report was sent, with its reference.

Screenshots are kept privately: only you and the SYNAPSE team can open them. A photo's location and camera details are removed when it's attached.

## Follow your reports

Open **Help** → **Your reports** on the web, or **Profile** → **Your reports** in the app. Each report shows where it stands:

| Status | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Received** | The team has it and hasn't started on it yet. |
| **Being looked into** | Someone on the team is working on it. |
| **Resolved** | It's fixed, or the team has answered it. |
| **Closed** | The team won't change anything — for example, it works as designed or was reported twice. |

When the team replies, the reply appears on the report. You're told in your notifications whenever a report moves on or gets a reply, and by email or desktop notification if you've turned those on. See [Notifications](/help/your-account/notifications).
