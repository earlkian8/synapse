## Access and signing in

### I can't find a screen, a button, or an article

SYNAPSE shows each person only what their role allows — the sidebar, the buttons on a page, the dashboard and this Help Center all follow your role. If something you need is missing, ask whoever manages roles in your workspace. See [Welcome to SYNAPSE](/help/getting-started/welcome-to-synapse).

### A page says I'm not allowed to do that

The action needs a permission your role doesn't include. Nothing was changed. Ask an administrator if you should have it.

### My verification code didn't arrive

Check your spam or junk folder, then choose **Send a new code** on the confirmation screen. Each new code cancels the one before, so use the latest.

### I forgot my password

Choose **Forgot your password?** on the sign-in screen. Only you can reset your password — your HR team can't see or set it.

### It says "This account is inactive"

An administrator in your company has deactivated your account. Ask them to reactivate it.

### I'm in two companies and can't see my other company's records

You're looking at one workspace at a time. Switch companies from the name at the top of the sidebar. See [Working in more than one company](/help/your-account/working-in-more-than-one-company).

### Every page sends me back to the Setup Guide

Your company's setup isn't finished yet, and you're the one who can finish it. Work through it, skip the steps you don't need, or choose to set things up later on its last page. See [Setting up your company](/help/getting-started/setting-up-your-company).

## Attendance and leave

### The times look wrong

Attendance times are shown on your **company's** clock, not your device's. If they look hours off for everyone, the company's time zone may be wrong — whoever manages the company profile can check it under **Company Setup** → **Company Profile**.

### My punch was refused

Your company's attendance policy decides where and how punches may be made — for example only on site, or only from the mobile app. The message says why. A refused punch records nothing, so ask HR to record the time for you. See [Clocking in and out](/help/workforce/clocking-in-and-out).

### I forgot to clock out

Ask your HR team or manager to correct the day. Depending on your company's policy, the day may have been closed automatically at the end of your shift and flagged for a manager to check.

### Why was I marked late, or absent?

Each day is judged against the shift that applied to you that day and your company's attendance policy — including any grace for lateness. Whoever reviews attendance can open the day to see exactly which schedule and policy it was judged by. If the day was judged by something that has since been fixed, they can re-apply the rules.

### My leave charged fewer days than I expected — or more

Leave charges **working days** only: weekends and your company's non-working holidays aren't counted, and a single day can be a half day if the leave type allows it.

## Records and setup

### Why can't I delete this permanently?

Some records are kept because other records depend on them: a leave type with requests, an award type that has been given, a work site that punches name, a schedule people are assigned to, a training program people enrolled in, a framework appraisals were scored on. **Archive** it instead — it disappears from use, and the history that refers to it stays intact.

### I changed a schedule or policy and past days didn't change

That's deliberate: every day keeps the rules it was judged by, so last month's figures don't move under your feet. To bring past days in line, re-apply the rules — see [Correcting and signing off attendance](/help/workforce/correcting-and-signing-off-attendance).

### A job posting says "Expired"

Its closing date has passed. It stops taking applications, and closes itself the following day. Edit it to set a later date if you want to keep it open.

## AI and predictions

### The assistant isn't on my screen

It's offered to people whose role covers at least one module it can read. Self-service on its own — clocking in and filing your own leave — doesn't include it.

### "The service is temporarily unavailable"

The prediction or AI service is busy or offline for a moment. Nothing was recorded or lost, and earlier results stay visible. Try again shortly.

### Desktop notifications don't appear

Turn them on under **System** → **Notifications** in each browser you use, then check that both your browser and your operating system allow notifications from SYNAPSE. See [Notifications](/help/your-account/notifications).

## Still stuck?

If the assistant is offered to you, ask it — it can look at your own access and point you to the right screen. Otherwise, contact whoever manages SYNAPSE in your company: they can see your access and the [activity logs](/help/administration/activity-logs).
