When someone leaves, **Offboarding** runs their exit: the reason and the last day, and a **clearance** checklist that each responsible department signs off — returning equipment, closing accounts, settling accounts. Completing the exit separates the employee.

## Everyone who is leaving

**Offboarding** in the sidebar lists exits in one table. The tiles count people in offboarding, flagged clearance items, people leaving in the next 14 days, and exits completed this month.

Each row shows the employee, exit type, status, clearance progress (signed off out of total, with any flags) and last working day — in red if that day has passed and the exit isn't finished. The list shows **Active** exits by default; filter by status, exit type and department, or search. **Export** downloads it.

## Starting an exit

1. Choose **Start offboarding**.
2. Pick the employee, the **exit type** — resignation, termination, retirement or end of contract — the notice date, the **last working day** (not before the notice date) and the reason.
3. Start it.

The exit begins with a clearance checklist from your [offboarding program](/help/company-setup/offboarding-programs), each item already routed to the department that signs it off — IT, Finance, HR, or the leaver's own department.

## Working through clearance

Open an exit to see its clearance checklist, grouped by department, each group with its signed-off count:

- **tick** an item to sign it off — your name and the time are recorded;
- **flag** an item that has a problem, with the reason (it shows in red);
- add items the template didn't have, edit or delete them;
- **Clear all** signs off every pending item in one department.

From the exit's menu you can also **add from template**, **clear all pending**, and **edit details**. **Export sheet** downloads the clearance as a sheet to print or file.

The exit moves from **Initiated** to **Clearance** by itself at the first sign-off or flag. Its clearance reads as **pending**, **in progress** or **cleared** — a flagged item keeps it from being cleared.

## Completing the exit

Choose **Complete exit** when clearance is done. You're asked to confirm, and told about any items still outstanding. Completing:

- sets the employee's status to match the exit — **Terminated** for a termination, **Resigned** for a resignation, a retirement or the end of a contract;
- records when the exit was completed.

**Reopen** brings a completed or cancelled exit back, and returns the employee to **Active**. **Cancel** stops an exit that isn't going ahead, also returning them to **Active**. Deleting an exit leaves the employee's status as it is.

> [!NOTE]
> An exit can't be started for someone already resigned or terminated, and each employee has at most one.

## Who can do what

Viewing exits, running them (starting, clearing, completing) and managing clearance templates are separate permissions.
