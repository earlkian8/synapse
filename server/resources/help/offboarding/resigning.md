If you decide to leave, tell HR through SYNAPSE: **Offboarding** → **My resignation** on the web, or **Profile** → **Resignation** in the app. Nothing about your employment changes until HR accepts it.

## Filing your resignation

1. Choose **File your resignation**.
2. Pick your **last working day**. It starts 30 days from today: the notice the Labor Code asks of an employee who resigns. You can pick an earlier day — HR may agree to it, or ask you to stay longer.
3. If you want to, say **why you are leaving**. Only the people who handle exits see it.
4. Send it. HR is told straight away.

While it waits, **Withdraw it** takes it back. You can file a new one later.

## When HR decides

- **Accepted** — your exit starts, with the last day HR agreed with you. You are told, and **My resignation** follows it from then on.
- **Declined** — HR says why. You can talk to them, and file again.

## Your exit

Once your exit has started, **My resignation** shows:

- your **last working day**, and how far off it is;
- your **clearance**, item by item: what each department signs off before you go, such as returning your laptop or settling a cash advance. A **flagged** item says what is still needed from you;
- your **exit interview**, when it is waiting for you.

## Your exit interview

A short survey goes out with your exit: why you are leaving, how you rate seven parts of your time here, whether you would recommend the company or come back, and three open questions. It takes about five minutes. Only the people who handle exits read your answers; your team can't. Answering it also signs off the exit-interview item on your clearance.

## Your Certificate of Employment

Once your exit is completed, **Download your Certificate of Employment** (in the app, **Open your Certificate of Employment**) gives you the certificate as a PDF: your name, your position, and the dates you worked, signed by the person your company names. Every copy carries the same certificate number.
