Two parts of **Offboarding** come from the people leaving: the **resignations** they file themselves, and their answers to the **exit interview**. Both sit beside **Exits** in the module's sections.

## Resignations

**Offboarding** → **Resignations** lists every resignation filed from the web or the app, the ones waiting for you first. Each shows when it was filed, the last day proposed and the **notice** that gives — in amber when it is less than the 30 days the Labor Code asks for. You are notified as each one comes in.

Open one to decide it:

- **Accept** — set the **last working day** you agreed and, if you like, the clearance checklist to start from (**Best match** picks one by department and exit type). Their exit starts as a resignation, its notice date the day they filed, and the exit interview goes out.
- **Decline** — say why. They are shown your note, and may file again.

If you start someone's exit yourself while their resignation is waiting, the resignation is settled with it.

## Exit interviews

A survey goes out with every exit except a termination; on an exit's page, the **Exit interview** tab sends one by hand, or **Stop waiting** waives one that won't come back. A leaver who hasn't answered after three days is reminded once.

**Offboarding** → **Exit interviews** reads the answers together, over the last 3, 6 or 12 months:

- how many were sent and answered, the average rating, and how many would recommend you or come back;
- **Why people leave** — the main reason each gave, most common first;
- **How leavers rate us** — the average for each of seven topics, and how many gave each score from 1 to 5;
- every survey of the period. A row opens the exit on its interview, with the person's own words.

> [!NOTE]
> Answers are kept with the exit. Anyone who can see exits can read them, so treat them as you would the reason for leaving.

## The papers

An exit's **Documents** menu gives you:

- the **clearance form** as a PDF — the checklist as the sheet that used to be walked round the departments, with a line for each to sign, at any point of the exit;
- the **Certificate of Employment** as a PDF, once the exit is completed. It is numbered the first time it is issued, and signed by the name and title set under [Company profile](/help/company-setup/company-profile) → **Documents**.
