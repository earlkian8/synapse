**System** → **User Management** lists everyone who can sign in to your workspace, with their roles and status. Most employees get an account by joining from the mobile app — see [Inviting your people](/help/getting-started/inviting-your-people). This screen is where accounts are reviewed and administered.

## The list

The tiles count total, active, inactive and unverified accounts, accounts added this month, and archived ones. Search by name, email or phone; filter by status and by role; sort any column. **Export** downloads the list with your filters.

Each account shows whether its email is confirmed, whether two-factor authentication is on, and when it last signed in.

## Adding accounts

- **Add user** creates one account. Fill in the person's details, choose their roles, and optionally add a photo. A password is optional — leave it empty and the person sets their own. A confirmation email is sent to their address.
- **Import** creates many at once from a CSV file. Download the **Template** to see the columns. Each row is checked on its own: good rows are created, and anything wrong is listed with the reason — with a downloadable error report. A role can be given by name in the file if your role allows you to assign roles. Passwords are never imported, and a file can hold up to 200 people.

## Changing an account

From a row's **⋯** menu you can view, edit, **activate** or **deactivate**, reset the password, archive, restore and delete permanently.

- **Deactivating** stops the person signing in at once — on the web and in the app — without losing anything. Reactivate them to let them back in.
- **Archiving** removes the account from your workspace; **restore** it from the archived list or the [Trash bin](/help/administration/trash-bin). **Delete permanently** can't be undone.

Tick several accounts to activate, deactivate, archive or assign a role to all of them at once.

## Rules that protect accounts

- **You can't lock yourself out.** Nobody can deactivate, archive or delete their own account.
- **You can't change someone with more access than you.** Their details, sign-in, status and account are left alone.
- **You can only give roles whose access you hold yourself.** See [Roles and permissions](/help/administration/roles-and-permissions).
- **An account shared with another company belongs to its owner.** If someone also works in another workspace, you can change only their roles here. Archiving them removes them from **your** workspace — their account, and their place elsewhere, stay.

When a rule stops a bulk action for some accounts, the rest go ahead and you're told how many were skipped and why.

## Who can do what

Viewing, adding, editing, archiving, restoring, deleting permanently, activating and deactivating, resetting passwords and exporting are separate permissions — and so is assigning roles.
