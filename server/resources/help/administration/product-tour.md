**System** → **Product Tour** shows how your people move through the tour of the app — on the web or in the mobile app — and where the ones who leave it stop.

## What the tour does

Everybody is offered the tour once, the first time they reach the app. When the tour gains stops (as it did for **Search**), somebody who took an earlier version is offered a short **What's new** of only the new stops, once. Anyone can replay the tour from **Help** in the top bar, or **Profile** in the app.

## Reading the numbers

Choose **Web app** or **Mobile app**, a tour version (the current one to start), and a period.

- **Runs started**, and by how many people.
- **Walked to the end**, **Skipped**, and **Left part-way** — a run with no answer an hour after it started, when the page or the app was closed.
- **Stop by stop**: for each stop, in the tour's order, how many runs **showed** it, the share that **reached** it, and how many runs ended there without finishing. A stop many people leave at is the one to look at.

A stop is shown only to the people who can use it — somebody with no access to Company Setup never sees its stop — so each stop's share is of the runs that showed it.
