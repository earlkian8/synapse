People with permission to send notifications can write to the whole company, to everyone with a role, or to one person — a policy update, a maintenance window, a holiday greeting. It arrives like any other notification: in the bell and notification centre, and by email or as a desktop notification for whoever has chosen those.

## Sending one

1. Open **System** → **Notifications** and choose **Send notification**.
2. Choose who receives it:
    - **Everyone** — all active users in your workspace;
    - **A role** — everyone who holds it;
    - **One person**.
3. Write a **title** and the details.
4. Choose its **importance**: **Info**, **Success**, **Warning** or **Critical**. It sets the colour and icon people see.
5. Optionally add a **link** to a page in SYNAPSE (for example `/leave`), so clicking the notification takes people there.
6. Choose **Send notification**.

Deactivated accounts don't receive it.

> [!TIP]
> Keep the title short enough to read in the bell, and put the detail in the body.
