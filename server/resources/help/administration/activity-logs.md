**System** → **Activity Logs** is your workspace's audit trail: a record of who did what, and when. SYNAPSE writes it as people work — hires and status changes, approvals, corrections, role changes, settings, and more. Nobody can add to it by hand.

## Reading the trail

The tiles count events in all, today, this week and this month, and how many were creations and deletions. Search the trail by description, event, person, record or IP address; filter by event type; sort by any column.

Open an entry to see:

- **who** did it (or _system_, for something SYNAPSE did on its own, such as closing the day's attendance);
- **what** they did, and a plain description;
- **what it was done to** — the record's name is kept even if the record is later deleted;
- **what changed**;
- **from where** — the IP address and browser;
- **when**.

> [!NOTE]
> Actions taken through the assistant are recorded too, marked **via assistant**, under the name of the person who asked. Reading a named person's record through the assistant is recorded as well.

## Exporting

**Export** downloads the trail with your current search and filters, as a CSV spreadsheet.

## Deleting entries

People with permission to delete logs can remove an entry, several at once, or everything with **Clear logs**. Deleted entries can't be recovered — export what you need to keep first.

> [!WARNING]
> The trail is how your company shows who did what. Many organisations keep it for a set period for audits; check your policy before clearing it.
