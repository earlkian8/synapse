**System** → **Trash Bin** gathers what has been **archived** across SYNAPSE in one place, to restore or to delete for good:

- user accounts;
- employees;
- departments;
- leave types.

You see the kinds you're allowed to view, and you can restore or delete a kind only if your role allows it on that kind's own screen. The bin never gives you more access than the screens do.

## Restoring

Choose **Restore** on an item and it goes back where it came from, as it was. Tick several to restore them together.

Some restores need a moment's thought: a restored account can sign in again, and a department or leave type whose code another one has taken since can't come back until one of the codes is changed.

## Deleting for good

**Delete permanently** removes an item and can't be undone. Deleting an employee permanently also removes their attendance, leave, appraisals and documents.

**Empty trash** deletes everything you're allowed to delete, after you confirm. Anything the Users screen wouldn't let you delete — your own account, an account shared with another company, or one with more access than yours — is skipped.
