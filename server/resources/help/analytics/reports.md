**Analytics & AI** → **Reports** turns the records spread across SYNAPSE into ready-made reports, each with charts, filters, an export and an optional AI analysis. A report's figures always match the screen they come from, because they are read from the same records.

## The reports

You see the reports your role allows — each needs the permission to view the module it reports on.

| Report                   | What it shows                                            |
| ------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Employee Masterlist**  | Everyone on your roster and their details                |
| **Headcount Summary**    | Headcount by department and employment type              |
| **Workforce Movement**   | Hires and separations over a period, and why people left |
| **Attendance Summary**   | Attendance for a period, person by person                |
| **Leave Ledger**         | Leave requested, taken and remaining                     |
| **Recruitment Pipeline** | Postings and where their candidates are                  |
| **Audit Trail**          | Who did what, and when                                   |

## Running a report

Pick a report from the list on the left; it opens alongside. Each one has its own filters — a date range, a month, a department, a status, a search — and changing one updates the report straight away. The address in your browser always reproduces exactly what you see, so you can bookmark it or send it to a colleague who has the same access.

Charts are drawn from the **whole** result, not only the page of rows on screen. Workforce and attendance reports also show the latest **prediction signals** — from attrition risk, promotion readiness and the performance forecast — each linking to its page.

## Exporting

**Export** downloads the full result as a CSV spreadsheet, with your current filters.

## AI insights

Choose **Generate insights** and an AI model reads the report's totals, charts and a sample of its rows, and answers in plain words: a headline, what's happening, what changed, why, and two to four recommended actions.

It reads what you've filtered — change a filter and you'll generate a fresh read for the new figures. If the AI service is busy, you'll see a message to try again shortly.

> [!TIP]
> If the assistant is offered to you, you can run reports from the chat too: "what's our turnover this year?"
