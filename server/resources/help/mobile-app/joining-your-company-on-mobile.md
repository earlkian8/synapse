The SYNAPSE mobile app is the employee companion: clocking in, your attendance, your leave, your profile and your awards, on your phone. Your company will tell you how to get it.

Getting started takes two steps: create **your own** account, then connect it to your employer.

## 1. Create your account

Open the app and choose **Create account**. Enter your name, an email address and a password. The account is yours — your employer doesn't set it up, can't see your password, and can't reset it for you.

## 2. Join your company

After creating your account, the app takes you to join a company. There are two ways in:

- **You were invited.** Invitations sent to your email address are listed — join with one tap. If you were invited at a different address, type the **8-character invitation code** from the email instead.
- **You have your company's join code.** Type the **7-character** code your HR team gave you. The app shows the company's name before you commit, and you choose **Continue**.

With a join code, you're admitted straight away if your email address matches your record in the company's directory. Otherwise your request **waits for HR to approve it** — the app shows it as pending, so you don't need to ask twice.

> [!NOTE]
> The same code box accepts either kind of code, so you don't need to know which you have.

## After you join

You start with self-service access: clocking in and out, and filing your own leave. The same email address and password also sign you in on the web.

## More than one company

If you work for more than one company, join each of them the same way. Switch between them from the company chip on **Home**, or the **Workspace** card on **Profile** — everything in the app then follows the company you've chosen.

## Forgot your password?

Reset it from the web sign-in screen with **Forgot your password?** — see [Signing in and out](/help/your-account/signing-in).
