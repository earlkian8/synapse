The **Clock** tab is your punch clock. It shows your company's time and today's shift, and one big button for the next thing to do:

**Time In** → **Start Break** → **End Break** → **Time Out**

Each punch records your **location**, and you can add a **selfie** — your company's policy may require one. Before a punch is sent, you're shown the time and where you are.

Below the button, the day so far: when you clocked in and out, and how long you've worked.

> [!NOTE]
> Times are always shown on your company's clock, not your phone's — so if you travel, you still see the same day your attendance is judged on.

## No signal? Keep punching

If your phone can't reach SYNAPSE when you punch — or earlier punches are still waiting — the punch is **saved on your phone** with the time you made it, and the button moves on as normal. A banner says how many punches are waiting, and the **Clock** tab shows a badge.

Waiting punches are sent automatically, oldest first, as soon as you're back online (and whenever you open the app). To send them straight away, choose **Send now**. Sending one twice does no harm — it's only recorded once.

Your company's policy sets how old an offline punch may be when it finally arrives. One sent too late is refused, with a message saying why — ask HR to record that time for you.

## If a punch is refused

The app tells you why — for example that you're outside your work site's fence, with the nearest site and how far away you are. A refused punch records nothing. Ask your HR team to record the time for you. See [Clocking in and out](/help/workforce/clocking-in-and-out) for the full list of reasons.

## Your attendance

The **Attendance** tab shows the month as a calendar, each day marked with its status, plus a summary: days present, late and absent, hours rendered, and late and overtime minutes. Tap a day to see its punches, with where each was made and whether it was sent offline.
