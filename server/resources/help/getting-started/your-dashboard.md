The dashboard is the first page you see after signing in. It brings together what is happening across your company today — but only the parts your role covers, so it looks different for an HR Manager, a Department Head and a member of staff.

## What it can show

Each block appears only when your role can see the module it comes from:

- **The pulse** — the band at the top: active staff, who is in today, leave waiting for review, and open roles.
- **Workforce** — how your people divide by employment type, the busiest departments, and headcount.
- **Attendance** — today's board, and the number of people present over the last 14 days.
- **Needs your attention** — one queue of everything waiting on you, such as leave to decide, attendance to sign off, overdue onboarding tasks, flagged clearance items and interviews coming up. Only items you are allowed to act on are listed, and an empty row is left out.
- **Coming up** — the next few events and meetings.
- **Recent activity** — the latest entries in the audit trail.

Every block links through to the screen it comes from.

## If your dashboard looks empty

A member of staff without management access sees **Your workspace** instead: shortcuts to the things you do for yourself and to your profile. That is expected — the company's figures are for the people whose role covers them.

> [!TIP]
> If the assistant is offered to you, ask it "what needs my attention today?" — it answers from the same figures the dashboard shows.
