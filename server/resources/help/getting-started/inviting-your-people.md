Adding someone to your employee records does not give them a way to sign in. People create their **own** SYNAPSE account in the mobile app, and then connect it to your company. After that, the same email and password also sign them in on the web. You can let them do that in two ways: send them an **invitation**, or give them your **company join code**. Both are managed on one screen: **Employees** → **App access**.

> [!NOTE]
> Nobody in your company can set or reset another person's password. The account is theirs; a forgotten password is reset by its owner from the sign-in screen.

## The App access screen

It is ordered by who is waiting on whom:

1. **Waiting to join** — people who used your join code but could not be matched to an employee record automatically.
2. **Invitations sent** — invitations not yet accepted, with their codes visible so you can read one out to somebody who never got the email.
3. **Not invited yet** — employees with no access, with **Invite** on each row and **Invite all**.

Each employee's row in [Employee records](/help/workforce/employee-records) also shows their access: **active**, **invited**, or none.

## Inviting someone

1. Make sure the employee's record has the right email address — the invitation goes there.
2. On **App access**, find them under **Not invited yet** and choose **Invite** (or **Invite all**).
3. They receive an email with a link and an 8-character code. Whoever holds the code can use it to join, so it does not need to match the address they sign up with.

**Resend** issues a fresh invitation and cancels the old one. **Revoke** cancels it outright. An invitation that is not accepted in time expires on its own.

## Using the join code

Your company has a 7-character join code, shown at the top of **App access**. Anyone who enters it in the app asks to join your company:

- if their account's email matches exactly one employee record that has no account yet, they are admitted straight away;
- otherwise they wait under **Waiting to join** until someone reviews them.

To admit someone who is waiting, choose **Approve** and pick the employee record they belong to — records whose email matches are listed first. **Decline** turns the request down.

People who manage the company profile can **turn joining by code off** and **generate a new code** (the old one stops working at once).

## What a new member can do

Everyone who joins starts with the **Staff** role: clocking in and filing their own leave. Give them more on the [User accounts](/help/administration/user-accounts) screen, if your role allows.
