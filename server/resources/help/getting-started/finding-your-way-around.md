SYNAPSE keeps the same layout on every page: the **sidebar** on the left for moving between screens, the **top bar** for things you need anywhere, and the page itself in the middle.

## The sidebar

The sidebar is grouped into sections, in the order work happens:

| Section                | What is in it                                                           |
| ---------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Main**               | Your dashboard                                                          |
| **Talent Acquisition** | Recruitment and onboarding                                              |
| **Workforce**          | Employees, attendance, leave, performance, training, awards, and events |
| **Offboarding**        | Exits and their clearance                                               |
| **Analytics & AI**     | Predictions and reports                                                 |
| **Company Setup**      | How your company runs: its structure, hours, policies and templates     |
| **System**             | Notifications, accounts, roles, the audit trail and archived records    |

You only see the screens your role can open, and a section with nothing in it for you is left out entirely. The company's name or logo at the top takes you to its profile (or your dashboard); if you belong to more than one company, it is a **workspace switcher** instead.

To make more room, collapse the sidebar to icons with the button at the left of the top bar. Hover over an icon to see its name. On a phone, the same button opens the sidebar as a panel.

## The top bar

From left to right:

- **Breadcrumbs** — where you are, with a link back up each level.
- **Search** — or **⌘K** / **Ctrl+K** from any page — finds people, records, screens and help articles. See [Searching SYNAPSE](/help/getting-started/searching-synapse).
- **Help** (the question mark) — this Help Center, help for the page you are on, the product tour, and — for whoever sets the company up — the Setup Guide.
- **Theme** — switch between light and dark.
- **Notifications** (the bell) — the latest alerts, with a dot when something is unread. See [Notifications](/help/your-account/notifications).
- **Your account** (your photo or initials) — your settings and signing out.

## The assistant

The **Assistant** button in the top bar — or **⌘J** / **Ctrl+J** from any page — opens the assistant beside the page: ask about your workspace in plain words, or ask it to do something for you. It is offered to people whose role covers at least one module it can read. See [Meet the assistant](/help/assistant/meet-the-assistant).

## Lists, records and forms

Most screens follow one pattern, so once you know one you know them all:

1. A **header** with the page's title and its main actions (for example **New posting** or **Export**).
2. A row of **stat tiles** — the numbers that matter on that screen.
3. A **toolbar** with search and filters, and **Reset** to clear them.
4. A **table**. Click a column heading to sort by it, click a row to open it, or use the **⋯** menu at the end of a row for everything else you can do to it.

Records and forms open as centred windows. Their buttons stay in view at the bottom however long the form is, and anything that cannot be undone asks you to confirm first.

> [!TIP]
> The **Help for this page** item in the Help menu opens the article about whatever screen you are on.

## The tour

The first time you reach the app, SYNAPSE offers a one-minute tour of the parts of the screen your role can use. You can skip it, and replay it at any time from **Help** → **Take the tour**.
