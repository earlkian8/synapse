SYNAPSE is the HR system your company runs on — from the first job posting to an employee's last day. Recruitment, onboarding, employee records, attendance, leave, appraisals, training, recognition, events and offboarding all live in one place, and they share one set of records, so a hire made in Recruitment is the same person whose attendance, leave and appraisals follow.

## Your company is your workspace

Everything you see belongs to one company — your **workspace**. Its people, their records, its settings and its history are kept entirely separate from every other company's. If you work for more than one company, you have one account and can switch between their workspaces; see [Working in more than one company](/help/your-account/working-in-more-than-one-company).

## You see what your role allows

Every person in a workspace holds one or more **roles**, and a role is a set of permissions — _view leave requests_, _approve leave_, _clock in_, and so on. SYNAPSE shows you only what your roles allow:

- the sidebar lists only the screens you can open;
- the dashboard shows only the figures you are allowed to see;
- this Help Center lists only the articles about screens you can open;
- the assistant can only see and do what you could do yourself.

So two colleagues can see quite different versions of SYNAPSE, and both are correct. Every company starts with three built-in roles:

| Role                | Who it is for                   | In short                                                                     |
| ------------------- | ------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **HR Manager**      | The company's owner and HR team | Everything: every module, analytics, setup and administration                |
| **Department Head** | Supervisors                     | Their team's records, leave approvals, appraisals, and read-only predictions |
| **Staff**           | Every employee                  | Self-service: clocking in and filing their own leave                         |

Your company can also create its own roles. If a screen you need is missing, ask whoever manages roles in your workspace.

> [!NOTE]
> The person who registers a company becomes its first HR Manager and is taken through the Setup Guide before anything else. See [Setting up your company](/help/getting-started/setting-up-your-company).
