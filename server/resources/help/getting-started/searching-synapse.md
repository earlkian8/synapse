Search finds what you are looking for without your needing to know which screen it lives on. Type a name, an employee number, a job title or what you want to do, and open the result.

## Opening search

- Click the **Search** field in the top bar, or the magnifying glass when the window is narrow.
- Or press **⌘K** on a Mac, **Ctrl+K** elsewhere, from any page. Press it again, or **Esc**, to close search.

Before you type anything, search lists the screens you can open, so it doubles as a quick way to jump around.

## What it finds

Results are grouped, always in the same order:

| Group              | Found by                                    | Opens                                  |
| ------------------ | ------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------- |
| **Screens**        | Their name, or what you go there for        | The screen                             |
| **Employees**      | Name, employee number, email or phone       | Their record                           |
| **Applicants**     | Name, email or headline                     | Their latest application, on its board |
| **Job postings**   | Title, description or requirements          | The posting's hiring board             |
| **Onboarding**     | The new hire's name or number               | Their checklist                        |
| **Offboarding**    | The leaver's name or number                 | Their clearance                        |
| **Leave requests** | The employee, or the leave type             | The request, ready to review           |
| **Attendance**     | A day, a month or a kind of day — see below | The day, open on the attendance board  |
| **Appraisals**     | The employee and the cycle — "maria q3"     | The appraisal                          |
| **Training**       | Program name or provider                    | The program                            |
| **Events**         | Title or location                           | The event                              |
| **Departments**    | Name or code                                | The department                         |
| **Users**          | Name or email                               | Their account                          |
| **Roles**          | Name or description                         | The role                               |
| **Help**           | Title, keywords and text                    | The article                            |

Every word you type has to match, so "maria santos" finds Maria Santos and not every Maria. The best five of each group are shown, closest match first.

### Finding attendance days

Attendance answers when your words say attendance: a day (**today**, **yesterday**, **2026-10-03**, **10/3**, **oct 3**), a month (**october**, **oct 2026**), a kind of day (**late**, **absent**, **undertime**, **overtime**, **on leave**, **holiday**, **incomplete**), or the word **attendance** itself. The rest of what you type is the person:

- **maria late** — Maria's late days, newest first;
- **santos oct 3** — Santos's day on 3 October;
- **absent today** — everyone absent today.

A name on its own finds the person, not a month of their days.

You only ever see what your role lets you open: a group you have no access to is not searched at all. Archived records are left out, and so is anything a person wrote as the reason for leave or for leaving.

## Moving through results

- **↑** and **↓** move between results, and **↵** (Enter) opens the one picked out.
- Click any result to open it.
- The words that matched are picked out in each result, so you can see why it was found.

A record that opens in a window over its list, such as an employee or a leave request, opens with that window already showing.

> [!TIP]
> If what you need is a question rather than a name, the last result is **Ask the assistant**, with your words already typed in. See [Meet the assistant](/help/assistant/meet-the-assistant).

## Searching in the mobile app

The app has its own search: the magnifying glass at the top of **Home**, or **Profile** → **Search**. It finds the app's screens as you type, and **your own** records — never anybody else's:

- your **leave requests**, by type, status or date ("sick leave", "pending");
- your **days**, by date or kind ("late in october", "oct 3");
- your **invitations**, by title or place;
- your **awards**, and the **holidays** you keep ("holy week").
