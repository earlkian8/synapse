When you register a new company, SYNAPSE takes you through the **Setup Guide** before anything else. It has one step for every Company Setup screen, in a sensible order, and each step carries that screen's full editor — so whatever you set up here is exactly what you would set up on the screen itself.

## The steps

The guide runs in three stretches:

| Stretch                       | Step                     | What you set up                                                |
| ----------------------------- | ------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Your company**              | 1 · Company              | Your company's name, details and time zone, and your join code |
|                               | 2 · Departments          | Your departments and the positions in them                     |
| **Time & attendance**         | 3 · Attendance rules     | How a working day is judged, and optionally a default schedule |
|                               | 4 · Schedules & holidays | Working hours and your holiday calendar                        |
|                               | 5 · Leave                | The kinds of leave you offer                                   |
|                               | 6 · Locations            | Your work sites and their clock-in fences                      |
|                               | 7 · Shift roster         | Who works which shift                                          |
| **Your people, hire to exit** | 8 · Hiring               | The stages your hiring runs through                            |
|                               | 9 · Onboarding           | The checklist new hires start from                             |
|                               | 10 · Appraisals          | What appraisals are scored against, and a review cycle         |
|                               | 11 · Awards              | The awards you give                                            |
|                               | 12 · Offboarding         | The clearance checklist for exits                              |

## Working through a step

Each step is laid out the same way:

1. **Suggestions**, where there are any — for example common departments, the Philippine holiday calendar, statutory leave types or ready-made hiring processes. Tick the ones you want; most can be **customised** before you add them, and every step lets you start from nothing instead.
2. **The editor** — the Company Setup screen itself. Anything you add appears here straight away, ready to rename, nest or extend.
3. **The footer** — **Back**, **Skip this step**, and one button forward. **Continue** needs the step to hold something; if you picked suggestions, the button says what it will add first (for example "Add 5 leave types and continue").

> [!TIP]
> Skipping is fine. Nothing is lost, the guide remembers which steps you skipped, and everything stays available from **Company Setup** in the sidebar.

A step for something your role cannot configure is still shown, marked **No access**, so you can see what is left for someone else.

## Finishing

The last page shows where every step landed, with **Review** or **Do it now** beside each. **Finish, and bring your people in** closes setup and takes you to inviting your employees — see [Inviting your people](/help/getting-started/inviting-your-people). Choosing to set things up later closes it too.

Until setup is closed, SYNAPSE brings you back to the guide whenever you open another page, so you are never left on an empty dashboard. Your own account settings stay reachable throughout.

## Picking up a skipped step later

Open **Company Setup** → **Setup Guide** in the sidebar, or **Help** → **Setup Guide** in the top bar. It opens where you left off.
