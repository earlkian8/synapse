Open the assistant with the **Assistant** button in the top bar, or press **⌘J** (Mac) or **Ctrl+J** (Windows) from any page. It opens as a panel at the right edge of the screen. On a wide screen the page moves over to make room, so nothing is covered. Ask it about your workspace in plain words and it answers from your live records. Or ask it to do something, and it does the work through the same rules as the screens.

It is offered to people whose role covers at least one module it can read. It sees and does only what **you** could see and do yourself.

## Asking questions

Type the way you'd ask a colleague:

- "What needs my attention today?"
- "How many people are on leave this week?"
- "Who hasn't clocked in yet?"
- "How is Maria Santos's onboarding going?"
- "Which candidates for the Accountant posting are the strongest?"
- "When is the next holiday?"
- "How do I set up a new leave type?"

Before answering, it reads the records your question is about, and shows you what it read above its reply, as a line of steps — for example "Read Maria Santos's record: Employee record · Leave (2026) · Attendance (last 30 days)" — so you can check an answer against its sources. An open circle is something it read; a filled one is something it changed; an amber one is waiting for your OK. A long list is folded into one line, such as "Read 4 sources and made 1 change"; select it to see every step. When a question is about a person whose name is shared by several people, it asks which one you mean.

## Getting things done

Ask it to act, and it uses the same tools as the screens:

- "Approve Ana's leave request for next week."
- "Schedule an interview with John Reyes for Thursday at 2pm."
- "Enroll the sales team in the negotiation workshop."
- "Chase everything overdue on Ben's onboarding checklist."
- "Give Carla the Customer Hero award for the Davao rollout."

Anything that matters — changes that notify people, can't easily be undone, or affect many records — waits for you: the assistant shows exactly what it is about to do, and nothing changes until you press **Confirm**. What it did is listed under its reply, one line per record, with what happened to each. See [What the assistant will and will not do](/help/assistant/assistant-privacy-and-confirmations).

## Attaching files

Attach up to eight files — PDF, image or text — with the paperclip, paste them, or drag them anywhere onto the panel. For example, attach a CV and ask it to add the person as a candidate.

## Your conversations

Conversations are kept, so you can pick one up later on any device. Select the conversation's name at the top of the panel to see the others, and to search, pin, rename or delete them. The pencil starts a new conversation, and the arrows make the panel wider for long replies and tables. You can copy a reply, edit a message you sent, or ask for the last reply to be written again. **Esc** closes the panel.

## If it can't answer

- **"You don't have access to that"** — the answer would need a permission your role doesn't have.
- **It's busy, or you've asked a lot in a short time** — there's a limit on how many messages can be sent per minute and per day. Wait a moment and use **Retry**.
- **It only does HR work.** Questions about anything outside your workspace are politely declined.
