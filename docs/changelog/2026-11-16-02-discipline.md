# Discipline, through the twin-notice rule

Incidents are now taken through due process in SYNAPSE: a notice to explain with at least
five calendar days to answer, the employee's written answer, a hearing when they ask for
one, and a written notice of decision, charged under the company's code of conduct with
penalties that climb with each repeat. Each employee has a conduct record. See
[ADR 0105](../decisions/0105-discipline-taken-through-the-twin-notice-rule.md).

## Highlights

- **No step skipped.** No decision without a notice; none before the answer is in or its
  due date passes; none while a hearing asked for in writing is still to be held; none
  without reasons; no dismissal on a company rule alone; preventive suspension capped
  at 30 days; nobody decides their own case.
- **The next step, always in view.** A case opens on what due process asks now and what
  blocks a decision, beside a dated timeline of every step.
- **Proof of service.** Employees acknowledge each notice in the app; each notice prints
  under the letterhead with a line to sign.
- **A code of conduct with a ladder.** Gravity, the Article 297 ground, and the penalty
  for each repeat within a period; the code's step is offered first at decision time.
  Fourteen standard offenses to start from.
- **Each to their part.** HR sees cases whole; supervisors report and follow status only;
  employees see what was served on them, never witnesses or evidence.
