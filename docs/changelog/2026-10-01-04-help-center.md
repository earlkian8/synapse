# A Help Center: the user manual, in the app

SYNAPSE now has a Help Center at `/help`: 54 step-by-step articles in 11 topics, covering
every screen and everyday task — from signing in and clocking in, through hiring, leave,
appraisals and offboarding, to attendance policies, roles and the assistant. It is
reached from **Help** in the top bar, which can also open the article for the page you
are on. Like the rest of the app, it shows each person only what their role can use. See
[ADR 0062](../decisions/0062-a-help-center-read-for-the-reader.md) and the
[Help Center](../modules/help-center.md) module doc.

## Highlights

- **A standard help-center layout.** The home has a search on the brand's navy band,
  *Start here* picked for the reader's role, a card per topic, and *Still need help?*.
  Topic and article pages use a documentation layout: topics down the left, the
  article in the middle, *On this page* on the right with the section being read lit
  up, related articles, and previous / next through the whole manual. On a phone the
  topics fold into a *Browse topics* panel.
- **Search as you type.** Results show the line that matched, with the searched-for
  words in bold. `/` focuses the search on any help page.
- **Help for this page.** The Help menu opens the article for whatever screen you are
  on — a posting's pipeline gets *Candidates and the pipeline*, not the postings list.
- **Only what your role can use.** Staff see 14 articles and an HR Manager all 54. An
  article about a screen you cannot open is not listed, not searchable and not served;
  a link to it inside another article keeps its words but stops being a link.
- **Ask the assistant from any article.** For people it is offered to, *Ask the
  assistant* opens it with the question started — never sent for you. The assistant's
  "how do I…" answers now include the matching article.

## Backend

- `Support\Help\HelpCenter`: the catalogue (11 categories, 54 articles, each with the
  permissions of the screen it explains) and its reads: `articles()`, `categories()`,
  `category()`, `article()` (with link pruning, reading time, related reading and
  neighbours), `search()` (weighted, complete matches first) and `forPath()`.
- Articles: `resources/help/<category>/<slug>.md`, with `[!NOTE]` / `[!TIP]` /
  `[!IMPORTANT]` / `[!WARNING]` callouts.
- `Help\HelpCenterController` with `Help\HelpSearchRequest` and
  `Help\HelpForPageRequest`, on `routes/help.php` (`help.index`, `help.search`,
  `help.for-page`, `help.category`, `help.article`) under `auth` + `verified`. An article
  the reader may not open is a 404. Not activity-logged.
- `Services\Assistant\AssistantAccess`: who is offered the assistant, moved from the
  browser; shared as `auth.assistant`.
- `SystemGuideModule::find_help` adds up to two Help Center cards when an article
  matches every word of the question; `SystemGuide` gains a `help-center` entry.

## Frontend

- New feature `features/help-center/` and pages `pages/help/{index,category,article,search}`.
  Articles render with react-markdown and remark-gfm (already installed) plus a small
  remark plugin for heading anchors and callouts.
- Help menu: *Help Center* and *Help for this page* above *Take the tour* and the
  *Setup Guide*. The tour's Help stop mentions the Help Center.
- `features/assistant/launcher.ts` opens the assistant with a question typed; the
  launcher is now gated on `auth.assistant`.
- The footer's *Docs* link opens the Help Center.
- **Sticky now sticks.** The app shell's content column used `overflow-x-hidden`,
  which made it a scroll container and quietly disabled `position: sticky` inside it —
  including the top bar, which scrolled away. It is `overflow-x-clip` now: the top bar
  stays put on every page, as it was meant to.

## Notes

- The articles were written from the module docs and checked against the screens' own
  labels. Changing a screen now includes changing its article; `HelpCenterTest` catches
  broken links, missing files and stale permissions, but not stale words.
- Two things the manual found: the dashboard's shortcuts for an employee without
  management access link to `/leave` and `/attendance`, which the built-in Staff role
  cannot open. Staff reach their time record at `/attendance/me` (the manual links
  there), and file leave from the mobile app. The shortcuts are unchanged here.
